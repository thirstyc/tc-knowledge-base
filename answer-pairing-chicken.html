Wine with chicken?

Short Answer

Chicken goes with almost anything, so pick the wine for the sauce and the cooking method, not the bird.

A simple roast chicken is one of the best dishes for wine. White Burgundy or another lightly oaked Chardonnay suits the crispy skin and juices, and a Pinot Noir or Beaujolais works if you prefer red.

Lemon, herbs and garlic call for something fresh and zesty: Sauvignon Blanc, Vermentino or a dry Riesling.

Creamy sauces and mushrooms want a fuller white with some texture, like oaked Chardonnay or Chenin Blanc from Vouvray or South Africa.

Coq au vin is traditionally made with red Burgundy, so drink the same.

Fried chicken and sparkling wine are a genuinely great match. The bubbles and acidity cut through the crispy coating and grease.

Barbecued, jerk or smoky grilled chicken can take a juicy red like Zinfandel or Grenache, or an off-dry Riesling if there's heat.

Spiced dishes like chicken tikka masala suit an aromatic, off-dry white such as Gewürztraminer, or a dry rosé.

Grilled chicken salad? Keep it light: a dry rosé or a crisp white.
