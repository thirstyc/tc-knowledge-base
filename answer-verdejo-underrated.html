Why don't more people know Verdejo?

Topic

Answers

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

Rueda gets overshadowed by Rioja and Ribera del Duero, Spain's red-wine headliners. It's one of Spain's best fresh whites and still cheap. The insider order at any Spanish restaurant.
