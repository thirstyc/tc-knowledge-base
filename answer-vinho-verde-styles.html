Is Vinho Verde always white?

Short Answer

No, that is the biggest misconception about it. Vinho Verde is a region, not a colour, and it produces white, red, and rosé versions, though white is what gets exported and what most people mean when they say the name. The reds, made from grapes like Vinhão, are deeply coloured, high in acid, and honestly a bit rustic, mostly consumed locally rather than shipped abroad. Rosé versions exist too but are minor players. So when a bottle just says Vinho Verde with no further detail, assume white unless the label tells you otherwise. The white blends, often built around Loureiro, Trajadura, or Arinto depending on the producer, are what built the region's reputation internationally. If you want to explore the reds, you will mostly need to be in Portugal to find them, which is arguably reason enough to go.
