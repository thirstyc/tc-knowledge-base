Chenin Blanc (South Africa)

South African Chenin. Similar to Loire but riper. Medium-high alcohol, high acidity. Mineral, fruity. Stellenbosch's strength. Proof Chenin travels well.

Also Called
Steen (South African name)
Typical Alcohol
12–14%
Acidity
High
Homes
Stellenbosch (South Africa)
