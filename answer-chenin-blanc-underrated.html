Why is Chenin Blanc so underrated?

Topic

Chenin Blanc

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

It doesn't have one signature style to hang a reputation on, unlike Sauvignon Blanc or Chardonnay. Vouvray, Savennières, and South Africa all do it differently. Versatility is the reason it flies under the radar.
