Garnacha

Spanish Grenache. High alcohol, soft tannin, generous. Medium-high acidity. Warm-climate grape. Same grape as Grenache but Spanish expression. Rustic, friendly, fun.

Also Called
Grenache (France), Cannonau (Italy)
Typical Alcohol
14–15%
Tannin
Low-Medium
Homes
Spain, Aragon, Priorat
