How Wine Works

Sulphites

Sulfur dioxide is the winemaker's essential preservative, added to prevent oxidation and unwanted microbial activity. Nearly all wine contains some, even wines labeled "no sulfites added," since fermentation itself produces trace amounts.

Function
Preservative, antioxidant, antimicrobial
Naturally Occurring
Yes, in trace amounts from fermentation
Low-Sulfite Wines
Most natural and orange wines
Headache Myth
Sulfites are rarely the real cause
