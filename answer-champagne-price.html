Why is Champagne so expensive?

Topic

Fermentation

Difficulty

Intermediate

Short Answer

Bottle fermentation takes years and skilled labor that tank-method sparkling wine skips entirely. Add centuries of prestige and legally protected scarcity, and the price follows.
