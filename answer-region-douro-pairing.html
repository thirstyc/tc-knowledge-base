What pairs with Douro reds?

Short Answer

Douro reds are built around touriga nacional, touriga franca, and tinta roriz, the same grapes that go into Port, and they carry over a lot of that structure: firm tannin, concentrated dark fruit, and enough grip to stand up to fat and char. That's why grilled sardines work despite sounding like an odd match on paper, the char and the oil in the fish need a wine with enough tannin to cut through, and these reds have it. Roast pork does the same job on a bigger scale, the fat rendering down against the wine's tannin so neither one dominates. Aged cheese is the third leg of that stool: the hard, salty, crystalline texture of something well-aged needs a wine with real backbone or it just gets flattened, and Douro reds don't flatten easily. Serve around 16 to 18°C, slightly cooler than room temperature, so the tannin doesn't come across as harsh. These are wines that want food, they're not built for sipping alone on a patio.
