What pairs with Savagnin?

Short Answer

Savagnin's walnut, curry-spice, and dried-apple notes come from oxidative aging under voile, so it needs food that can stand up to that intensity rather than get bulldozed by it. Comté is the classic match for a reason: the cheese is aged in the same region, under similar oxidative logic, and the nutty flavors reinforce each other instead of competing. Shellfish, especially something with a bit of richness like lobster or scallops, works because the wine's acidity cuts the fat while the savory depth matches the sweetness of the seafood. It's also one of the rare whites that handles curry and other warm-spiced dishes gracefully, since the wine already tastes faintly of curry itself. Avoid anything delicate or floral, it will get flattened. Serve slightly warmer than you would a crisp white, around 12 to 14°C, to let the oxidative aromatics show properly instead of hiding under chill.
