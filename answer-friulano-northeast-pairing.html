What pairs with Friulano?

Short Answer

Friulano's whole personality is built around cutting through fat and echoing savory, herbal flavors, so lean into that rather than fighting it. Seafood is the obvious match, especially anything with olive oil and herbs, grilled branzino, clams, a simple seafood risotto, because the wine's acidity resets your palate between bites and its almond-tinged finish plays nicely with brininess. Fresh pasta with herb-forward sauces, sage butter, pesto, works for the same reason: the wine's own herbal notes don't compete, they harmonize. Soft, young cheeses like a fresh Montasio or a mild goat cheese are good too, since the wine has enough acid to cut the creaminess without a lot of tannin to clash with. Avoid anything heavily spiced or very rich and buttery, it'll flatten the wine's more delicate herbal side. Serve it cool, around 10 to 12 degrees, and drink it young, within a couple of years of the vintage, before that fresh green character starts to fade.
