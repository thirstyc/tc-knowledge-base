How should I serve Pajarete?

Short Answer

Serve it cool, around 10 to 12°C, not room temperature and definitely not warm. Because it's rich and sweet with real alcohol behind it (often 15 to 18%), warmth makes it feel heavy and cloying instead of layered. A small glass is the move, a sherry copita or a small white wine glass, not a big bowl that lets the aromas blow off before you get to smell anything. Pour a modest amount, 60 to 90 ml, since this isn't a wine you drink by the glassful. Once opened, keep the bottle in the fridge; the sugar and alcohol both help preserve it, so it can last weeks rather than days, slowly losing a bit of freshness but staying perfectly drinkable. Don't chill it icy cold either, that just mutes the raisin and toffee notes you're paying for. Treat it the way you'd treat a good tawny port or PX sherry: cool, small pours, and no rush. It's built for lingering, not shooting back.
