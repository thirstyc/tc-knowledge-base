What pairs with Mencía?

Short Answer

Mencía's whole appeal is freshness and light tannin, so it wants food that won't bury that delicacy. Grilled fish like sardines or mackerel works well, especially with a bit of char, since the wine's acidity cuts through oil without overwhelming the fish. It's also a natural with mushrooms - a wild mushroom risotto or roasted porcini pick up on the earthy, savory undertone many Mencías get from slate or granite soils. Lighter meats like roast chicken, duck, or pork tenderloin give it enough substance to play against without the tannin having to fight fat and protein the way a bigger red would need to. Galician and northwestern Spanish cooking is the obvious pairing partner, since the wine and the food evolved together: think octopus, cured meats, or a simple tortilla. Serve it around 14-16°C, cool enough to keep the red fruit and acidity crisp. Avoid heavy, rich sauces or aged hard cheeses - they'll flatten the wine's brightness, which is really the whole point of drinking it in the first place.
