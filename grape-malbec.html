Malbec

Dark, structured, earthy. Cahors home before Argentina claimed it. Altitude changes everything—3,000ft in Mendoza gives you ripeness AND structure. The new world got this right.

Also Called
Côt, Pressac
Typical Alcohol
13–15%
Tannin
High
Homes
Cahors (France), Mendoza (Argentina), Sud-Ouest (France)

Malbec — What it is

Argentina's darling. When it left Bordeaux, found true home in Mendoza. Bold, structured, value-driven.

Malbec — Old World

Traditional expression, soil-forward, age-worthy.

Malbec — New World

Riper fruit, higher alcohol, experimental.

Malbec — Teaching moment

Malbec teaches you that same grape ≠ same wine across regions.
