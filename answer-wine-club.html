What is a wine club?

Short Answer

A wine club is usually a subscription that sends you a selection of wines regularly, from a winery, a retailer or an online service. The term can also mean a group of friends who meet to taste wine together.

Subscription clubs come in a few types:

  • Winery clubs send a producer's own wines, often including releases you can't buy elsewhere, and usually offer discounts and events.
  • Retailer and online clubs send curated mixed selections from different producers, often with tasting notes.
  • Themed clubs focus on a region, style, natural wine or rare bottles.

What to check before joining:

  • Cost per bottle compared with buying the same wines yourself.
  • Flexibility: can you skip shipments, swap wines or cancel easily?
  • Curation: do they explain why they chose each bottle?
  • Shipping: delivery costs, and whether they can legally ship to where you live, which varies a lot by country and region.

A social wine club, meanwhile, is easy to start: a few friends, a rotating host, a theme each time, and everyone brings a bottle. It's one of the best ways to taste widely without buying everything yourself.

Either way, the best clubs help you discover wines you wouldn't have picked alone.

Does wine have carbs?

Yes, but not many in dry wine: roughly 2–4 grams per 150 ml glass.
