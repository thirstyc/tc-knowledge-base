What pairs with Chilean Sauvignon Blanc?

Short Answer

This is a wine built for acid-driven, briny, herby food, not cream sauces or heavy roasts. Ceviche is the obvious match since the citrus and salt in the dish mirror the wine's own citrus-and-sea-air character, and ceviche's acidity won't clash with the wine's. Raw oysters work for the same reason, the wine's zip cuts through the brine without fighting it. Fresh, unaged cheeses like a young goat cheese or feta play well too, since the wine's acidity balances the cheese's tang instead of getting flattened by fat, the way it would with something aged and buttery. Light pasta with lemon, herbs, or a simple olive oil base is another easy fit, especially anything with cilantro or parsley, which tends to pick up the wine's grassy, herbal notes. Avoid heavier cream-based dishes or oily fish, both will make the wine taste thin. Keep it chilled and drink it young, this isn't a wine built for the cellar.
