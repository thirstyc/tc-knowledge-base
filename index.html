ANS-0142 · Grenache Why Grenache Gets You Tipsy Without Grabbing Your Gums

Sugar lives in the pulp, tannin lives in the skins. Grenache ripens fat and sweet with skins thin enough to keep the finish soft.

Enthusiast Intermediate ANS-0138 · Grenache Warm Grenache, Cool Grenache: Same Grape, Different Wine

Heat decides how ripe the fruit gets and which flavours turn up. Warm sites give dark fruit and more alcohol; cooler ones stay red-fruited and light on their feet.

Curious Drinker Beginner ANS-0129 · Grenache Is Old-Vine Grenache Worth The Money?

Often, yes — old vines give less fruit and more concentration. But age alone guarantees nothing. Who farmed it and where matters more than the number on the label.
