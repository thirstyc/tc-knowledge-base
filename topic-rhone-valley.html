Wine Region / France

Rhône Valley

Syrah country in the cooler north, Grenache-based blends in the warm south. Home to Châteauneuf-du-Pape and Hermitage, and some of France's best-value reds.

Key Grapes
Syrah, Grenache, Mourvèdre, Viognier
Climate
Continental north, Mediterranean south
Known For
Châteauneuf-du-Pape, Hermitage, Côtes du Rhône
Homes
Northern and Southern Rhône, southeastern France
