What pairs with Merlot?

Short Answer

Merlot's tannins ripen early and stay relatively soft and round, so it doesn't need fatty, protein-heavy food to tame it the way Cabernet Sauvignon does. That's what makes it so flexible at the table. Roast chicken, pork tenderloin, mushroom risotto, even a burger without too much char all work because the wine isn't fighting to soften itself against the food - it's just adding fruit and structure. Mild, semi-soft cheeses are a good match too, since Merlot's moderate acidity won't clash with cream. Where it struggles is with very fatty or intensely spiced dishes: it lacks the grip and acid backbone of a Cabernet or a Sangiovese to cut through heavy fat, and can taste flabby next to anything really rich. Serve it around 16-18°C, slightly cooler than room temperature - too warm and the alcohol and soft tannin turn syrupy. Merlot is the red for the person who says they don't like tannic wine but still wants something with weight.
