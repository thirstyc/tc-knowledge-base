Why does South African Shiraz fly under the radar?

Short Answer

Cabernet and Pinotage get most of the attention paid to South African reds, leaving this peppery, structured Shiraz in the background.

Why does South African Shiraz taste so powerful and peppery?

The pepper note isn't a metaphor, it's a real compound called rotundone, present in the grape skins and concentrated by South Africa's warm, sunny growing conditions, particularly in regions like Swartland and Stellenbosch.
