Why is Georgia suddenly trendy in wine circles?

Topic

Short Answer

Considered the birthplace of wine, with archaeological evidence going back thousands of years. Natural and orange wine movements rediscovered qvevri as the original amphora technique. Ancient tradition now reads as cutting-edge.
