Why does Croatian Plavac Mali taste so powerful and dark?

Short Answer

Steep, sun-baked vineyards on the Dalmatian coast, some of the hottest, most extreme sites in Europe. High alcohol, dense fruit, serious tannin. A rustic relative of Zinfandel, sharing ancestry through Crljenak Kaštelanski.
