What pairs with Ripasso?

Short Answer

Ripasso has real tannin and a dried-fruit richness that needs fat and protein to balance it, otherwise it can taste heavy on its own. Grilled or braised meats are the obvious match: a rib steak, lamb chops, short ribs, anything with charred exterior and enough fat to soften the tannin's grip. Heavier pasta works too, particularly ragù-based sauces or anything with mushrooms, since the wine's spice and dried-fruit notes echo umami flavors well. Aged hard cheeses, think a well-aged Grana Padano or Asiago, also hold their own against it. Avoid anything delicate or acidic like a simple tomato salad or fresh seafood, the wine will flatten it. Serve at 16 to 18 degrees, a touch warmer than a basic Valpolicella, so the wine's weight and spice come through instead of getting muted by cold. This isn't a wine for sipping solo on a patio, it wants a plate in front of it.
