Red Grape / France, Spain

Grenache

Soft, generous, high in alcohol and low in grip. It's the backbone of Châteauneuf-du-Pape, the whole point of Priorat, and the reason cheap rosé from the south of France tastes like strawberries. Garnacha if you're in Spain.

Also Called
Garnacha, Cannonau
Typical Alcohol
14–16%
Tannin
Low
Homes
Rhône, Priorat, Sardinia
