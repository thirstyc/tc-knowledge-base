Why does Georgia matter?

Topic

Answers

Difficulty

Short Answer

8,000 years of continuous winemaking, the oldest on record. Qvevri method never died out. Saperavi and Rkatsiteli exist nowhere else at this scale. This isn't a trend. It's where the whole thing started.
