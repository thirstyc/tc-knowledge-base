What pairs with Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc?

Short Answer

Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc is built around a specific acid-driven, herbaceous profile, think passionfruit, gooseberry, and that signature grassy or bell pepper note from compounds called methoxypyrazines, and the trick to pairing it is matching that intensity rather than fighting it. Fresh oysters and other raw shellfish work because the wine's high acidity does the same job a squeeze of lemon would, cutting through brininess and cleansing the palate between bites. Goat cheese is the classic match for a reason, the wine's zip slices through the cheese's tangy, chalky richness without either side overwhelming the other. Green salads with vinaigrette, herbs like cilantro or basil, and vegetable-forward dishes, asparagus especially, all echo the wine's own herbal streak instead of clashing with it. Avoid heavy cream sauces or richly oaked, buttery dishes, the wine has almost no oak influence itself and will taste thin against that kind of weight. Serve it cold, around 8 to 10°C, to keep that zing front and center.
