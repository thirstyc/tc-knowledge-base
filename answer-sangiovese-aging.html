Can You Drink Young Sangiovese or Does It Need Age?

Short Answer

Chianti is drinkable at 3–4 years (when released). Brunello needs 5+ years minimum. Vino Nobile sits in between. High tannin = needs time. You CAN drink young, but you'll regret it. It's harsh, angular, closed. Age reveals the beauty.
