Wine Tasting Framework — Overview

  1. Look — color tells age/oxidation
  2. Smell — primary (grape), secondary (fermentation), tertiary (age)
  3. Taste — attack, mid-palate, finish
  4. Structure — acidity, tannins, alcohol, body

Wine Tasting Framework — Practice

Taste the same wine three times. Different glasses change perception.
