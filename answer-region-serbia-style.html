Why does Serbian red taste so structured?

Topic

Tannins

Difficulty

Intermediate

Short Answer

Vojvodina's continental extremes — brutal winters, hot summers — build thick skins and firm tannin. Šumadija's heritage grapes add a rustic, earthy backbone you won't find in a supermarket blend.
