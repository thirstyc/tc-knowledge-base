Why is Alsace an outlier in France?

Topic

Answers

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

German grape varieties on French soil, thanks to a border that's changed hands more than once. Labels name the grape, not just the place — rare for France. A region that plays by different rules and gets away with it.
