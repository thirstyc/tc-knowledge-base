Wine with pie?

Short Answer

It depends on the filling. Sweet fruit pies want a dessert wine at least as sweet as the pie; savoury pies want a wine matched to the meat or vegetables inside.

Sweet pies:

  • Apple pie: late-harvest Riesling, sweet Chenin Blanc from the Loire, or Tokaji. Their apple and honey flavours and bright acidity suit the fruit.
  • Pecan pie: Tawny Port, sweet Madeira or Pedro Ximénez Sherry.
  • Pumpkin pie: Tawny Port, Vin Santo or a late-harvest white with spice.
  • Cherry or berry pie: Ruby Port, Brachetto d'Acqui or a sweet sparkling red.
  • Lemon meringue: Moscato d'Asti or a sweet Riesling, with acidity to match the lemon.
  • Chocolate cream pie: Ruby Port or Banyuls.

Savoury pies:

  • Steak and ale or beef pie: a hearty red like Syrah, Malbec or a Rhône blend.
  • Chicken pie in cream sauce: Chardonnay or a light Pinot Noir.
  • Pork pie: Beaujolais or a dry Riesling.
  • Fish pie: white Burgundy, Chablis or Chenin Blanc.
  • Tourtière, the spiced meat pie: Pinot Noir, Gamay or a Côtes du Rhône.

Pastry adds richness, so a wine with good acidity keeps each bite fresh.
