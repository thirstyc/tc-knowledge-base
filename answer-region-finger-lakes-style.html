Why does Finger Lakes Riesling taste so crisp and precise?

Topic

Riesling

Difficulty

Intermediate

Short Answer

Deep glacial lakes moderate brutal upstate New York winters, letting Riesling ripen slowly. High acid, laser-focused fruit, rarely heavy-handed with sugar. One of the coldest serious wine regions in the US.
