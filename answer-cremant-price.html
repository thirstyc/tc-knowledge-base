Why is Crémant cheaper than Champagne?

Short Answer

The production method is identical: second fermentation in the bottle, lees aging, hand riddling or mechanical equivalent, the whole traditional method process that makes Champagne taste like Champagne. What's different is the address. Champagne is a legally protected name tied to one specific region in northern France, and land there costs a fortune because everyone wants a piece of it, chalky soils, cool climate, centuries of marketing. Crémant comes from other French regions, Loire, Alsace, Burgundy, Jura, Limoux, where vineyard land is cheaper and the name carries none of that built-in prestige, even when the winemaking discipline is just as rigorous. You're not buying a lesser process, you're buying the same process without the postal code tax. Serve it the same way too, well chilled, around 8°C, in a white wine glass rather than a flute so the aromas have room to open up. If you're pouring bubbles for a Tuesday rather than a wedding, Crémant is the smarter bottle almost every time.
