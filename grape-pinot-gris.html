Pinot Gris

New Zealand style: medium-full body, low acidity, off-dry. Riper than French or Italian versions. Fruit-forward. The bridge between dry and sweet.

Also Called
Pinot Grigio (Italy), Grauburgunder (Germany)
Typical Alcohol
12–14%
Acidity
Medium
Homes
Central Otago (New Zealand)
