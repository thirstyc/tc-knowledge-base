White Grape / Italy

Vermentino

Fresh, mineral, coastal. Low alcohol, high acidity. Sardinian/Tuscan coastal white. Drinks young, pairs with seafood, tastes like Mediterranean salt air. The definition of summer in a glass.

Also Called
Vermentino di Sardegna
Typical Alcohol
12–13%
Acidity
High
Homes
Sardinia (Italy), Tuscany
