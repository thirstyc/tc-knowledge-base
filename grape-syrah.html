Red Grape / France, Australia, Argentina

Syrah

Peppery in the north, fruity in the south, savory everywhere. Altitude defines it—cool sites give finesse, warm sites give power. The same grape, completely different wines.

Also Called
Shiraz (Australia), Hermitage (Rhône), Côte-Rôtie (Rhône)
Typical Alcohol
13–15%
Tannin
High
Homes
Rhône (France), Australia, Argentina, South Africa

Syrah — What it is

Bold statement. Peppery, dark fruit, high tannin. New World ripeness vs Old World elegance.

Syrah — Old World

Traditional expression, soil-forward, age-worthy.

Syrah — New World

Riper fruit, higher alcohol, experimental.

Syrah — Teaching moment

Syrah teaches you that same grape ≠ same wine across regions.
