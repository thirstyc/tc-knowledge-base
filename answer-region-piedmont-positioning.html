Why do collectors chase Piedmont over Tuscany?

Topic

Answers

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

Barolo and Barbaresco are considered Italy's most age-worthy reds, the "wine of kings." Small production, cult-like devotion. Quieter fame than Tuscany, deeper respect among serious drinkers.
