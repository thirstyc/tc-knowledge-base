What pairs with Sciaccarello?

Short Answer

This is a light, high-acid, low-tannin red, so it wants food that's delicate rather than food that needs taming. Grilled fish works because the acidity cuts through any richness without fighting the fish's flavour the way a heavier red would. Charcuterie and Corsican specialties like wild boar stew or brocciu-based dishes are the classic local match, and it makes sense: island cuisine and island wine were built around each other, so the pairing is less a discovery than a long-standing habit. Mushrooms, especially earthy sautéed ones, pick up on the same stony, savoury undertone the granite soils give the wine. Avoid anything too fatty or too sweet, it will just make the wine taste thin. Serve it around 14-16C, slightly cooler than a typical red, so the fruit stays bright and the acid doesn't turn sharp. Think of it as a wine for a long lunch outside, not a heavy winter dinner.
