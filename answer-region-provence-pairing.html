What pairs with Provence rosé?

Short Answer

Provence rosé is made for acidity and restraint, not weight. Cool Mediterranean nights and sea breezes keep the grapes, mostly Grenache, Cinsault, and Syrah, from ever getting flabby, and the short skin contact that gives it that pale color also means almost no tannin. So it wants food that's light but flavorful, not rich or heavy. Grilled fish or shellfish, where the acid cuts through any oil and doesn't compete with delicate flesh. A Niçoise salad, with its olives, anchovy, and vinaigrette, matches the wine's savory, herbal undertone. Garlic aioli, tapenade, anything with a Mediterranean accent works because the wine grew up next to the same ingredients. Serve it cold, 8 to 10°C, colder than most white wine drinkers expect, because warmth exposes any flatness fast. This isn't a wine to overthink pairings for. It's built for outdoor eating in heat, and it does its best work fifteen minutes into a long lunch.
