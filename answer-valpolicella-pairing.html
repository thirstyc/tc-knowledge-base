What pairs with Valpolicella?

Short Answer

Valpolicella's whole appeal is soft tannin and bright acidity, which makes it flexible rather than fussy at the table. Its sour-cherry fruit and slight bitterness cut through tomato-based pasta sauces without fighting them, and the low tannin means it won't clash with lighter proteins the way a bigger red would. Grilled or roast chicken works, as does charcuterie, mild salami, and soft cheeses like taleggio or a young pecorino. It's also one of the few reds that handles a green salad with vinaigrette decently, since the acid in the wine matches the acid in the dressing instead of tasting flat next to it. Pizza is an obvious match too, especially anything topped with mushrooms or cured meat. Keep it simple: this isn't a wine for braises, game, or heavy sauces, it'll just get lost. Serve it slightly cool, around 13-14°C, and treat it as the house red for casual weeknight cooking rather than a special-occasion bottle.
