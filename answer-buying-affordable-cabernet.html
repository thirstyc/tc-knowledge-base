Best affordable Cabernet Sauvignon?

Short Answer

Look to Chile, Washington State, South Australia and southern France, where Cabernet Sauvignon ripens easily and land costs less than in Napa or Bordeaux.

The best value sources:

  • Chile, especially the Maipo and Colchagua valleys: ripe blackcurrant with a herbal, minty edge, and the most consistent value in the world.
  • Washington State: polished, structured Cabernet at prices far below Napa's.
  • South Australia: Coonawarra's red-soil Cabernet has a distinctive mint and eucalyptus note; Langhorne Creek and McLaren Vale are good too.
  • Languedoc and southwest France: Cabernet-based blends, often labelled Pays d'Oc.
  • South Africa: Stellenbosch Cabernet and Bordeaux-style blends.
  • Paso Robles and Lodi: California Cabernet at friendlier prices.

Is cheap, big-brand Cabernet worth it? Usually not the best use of money. The cheapest mass-market bottles are often softened with residual sugar and oak flavouring. Spending a little more on a smaller producer from one of the regions above gives much more character.

Serve affordable Cabernet with steak, burgers, lamb or hard cheese. A short decant helps even young, inexpensive bottles.
