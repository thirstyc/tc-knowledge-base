Why does Rheingau Riesling taste so structured compared to Mosel?

Topic

Riesling

Difficulty

Intermediate

Short Answer

Warmer, more sheltered stretch of the Rhine, often fully dry rather than off-dry. Riper fruit, firmer body, still built on serious acid. Germany's more powerful, more food-driven Riesling.
