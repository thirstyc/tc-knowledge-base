What pairs with Margaret River Cabernet?

Short Answer

Margaret River Cabernet is polished in a way that can make it seem lighter than it is, so it needs food with enough fat and structure to meet it head on. Lamb rack, especially with a herb crust, is the classic match: the wine's cedar and blackcurrant notes and firm, cool-climate tannin cut through the fat while the char on the meat mirrors the wine's own savoury edge. Aged gouda works for the same reason cheddar does with other Cabernets, the crystalline, salty texture softening tannin into something rounder. Roast beef, simply seasoned, gives the wine room to show its fruit without competing flavours getting in the way. What you want to avoid is anything too delicate or too spicy, since this is a wine with real backbone from Margaret River's gravelly, well-drained soils and maritime climate. Serve at 16 to 18 degrees and decant a young bottle for 30 to 45 minutes to let the tannins unwind.

Why does Loire wine taste so fresh and mineral?

The Loire is a long, cool river valley running west to east, and that cool climate is the whole story: grapes ripen slowly, holding onto malic acid instead of burning it off the way they would in a hotter region, so nearly everything made here comes out fresh rather than heavy.
