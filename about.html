Wine Knowledge Without The Gatekeeping

Most wine writing is either a textbook or a sales pitch. This is neither. It’s the answers we’ve had to look up ourselves, written the way we’d explain them across a table.

Who This Is For

People who order wine regularly, host dinners, maybe travel to wine regions, and still feel a flicker of doubt handing back the list. You don’t need credentials. You need someone to tell you what’s actually going on in the bottle.

How An Answer Gets Here

Every answer starts as a real question, from a reader, a dinner, or a shop. It gets researched against producers, texts and people who make the stuff, then written down in plain language and tagged so you can find it again.

Each one carries a confidence level, because some of this is settled chemistry and some of it is genuinely contested. When something changes — a law, a classification, a consensus — the answer gets re-verified and the date on it moves.

Who Writes It

Catherine Gignac, plus the people she drinks with. Thirsty Cunt also publishes on Substack, makes a blind tasting card game, and is building Thirsty Cellar — the app that answers in conversation rather than in articles.
