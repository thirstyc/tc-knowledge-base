What pairs with Viognier?

Short Answer

Because Viognier has so little acid to lean on, food pairing works differently than it does with a zippy white. You're not looking for something acid can cut through — you're matching richness with richness, and aroma with aroma. Roast chicken with the skin left on, salmon with a beurre blanc, a creamy risotto — anything with enough fat and body to meet the wine on its own terms works. Its stone-fruit and floral aromatics also do well against food with warm spice: Moroccan-spiced poultry, or dishes built around ginger and cardamom. Where Viognier struggles is anything sharp or acidic — a squeeze of lemon or a vinaigrette will make it taste flat and blowsy by comparison, since it has no acid of its own to answer back. Serve it around 10-12°C, not fridge-cold, so the texture and aromatics can show properly. It's a good bridge wine for people who claim they only like reds, since the weight on the palate is closer to a light red than a typical crisp white.
