Can You Drink Young Tannat or Does It Need Age?

Topic

Tannins

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

You CAN, but your gums will hurt. It's aggressively tannic young. Age it 10+ years and it opens up. Patience pays off. But most people don't age it. Instead, they pair it with rich food that tames the tannin.
