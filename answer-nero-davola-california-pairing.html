What should I actually serve with California Nero d'Avola, and at what temperature?

Short Answer

Serve it at 16 to 17 degrees, slightly cooler than room temperature, because the ripe fruit character can taste jammy and flat if it's too warm. A quick 15 minutes in the fridge before pouring does the job. On the plate, lean into things with fat and char rather than delicate flavors, since the wine's soft tannins and moderate acidity need something to cut through. Grilled sausage, a tomato-based pasta with sausage or eggplant, or a burger with charred onions all work because the acid in the wine handles the fat while the dark fruit matches the caramelized, smoky notes from the grill. Avoid pairing it with anything too light or delicate, like plain white fish, since the wine will just steamroll it. This isn't a wine that needs decanting for hours either, thirty minutes open is plenty since the tannins are already fairly resolved. Basically, treat it like a slightly fancier version of what you'd pour with a weeknight red sauce dinner.
