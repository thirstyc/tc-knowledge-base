Wine with caramel?

Short Answer

A sweet wine with nutty, toffee or dried-fruit flavours: fortified wines like Tawny Port, sweet Madeira and Pedro Ximénez Sherry, or Sauternes and late-harvest wines. The wine must be at least as sweet as the dessert.

That's the key rule with any dessert. If the dessert is sweeter than the wine, the wine tastes thin, sour and bitter, so dry wines don't work with caramel.

Good matches:

  • Tawny Port: its caramel, nut and dried-fruit notes echo the dessert perfectly.
  • Sweet Madeira, like Bual or Malmsey: rich, tangy and nutty, with acidity that keeps it fresh.
  • Sauternes or Barsac: honeyed and apricot-scented, ideal with crème brûlée and caramel custards.
  • Pedro Ximénez Sherry: very sweet and raisiny, for intense sticky toffee pudding or caramel ice cream. Some people pour it straight over the ice cream.
  • Tokaji Aszú: sweet but with bright acidity, lovely with caramelised apple or tarte Tatin.
  • Vin Santo: nutty and dried-fruit sweet, good with caramel and nut tarts.

Salted caramel is especially good with Tawny Port or sweet Madeira, since salt and sweetness play off each other.

Serve dessert wines slightly chilled, and in small glasses. A little goes a long way.
