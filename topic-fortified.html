How Wine Works

Fortified Wine

Wine with grape spirit added, which pushes the alcohol to roughly 15–22% and changes how the wine ages and keeps. Add the spirit during fermentation and the wine stays sweet; add it after, and the wine is dry.

Fortified During Fermentation
Port, Banyuls, Rutherglen Muscat — sweet, because sugar is left behind
Fortified After Fermentation
Sherry, most Madeira styles — dry base wine
Shaped By Air
Tawny Port, Oloroso Sherry and Madeira are deliberately oxidised; Fino ages under a yeast film called flor
Keeps Once Opened
Days to weeks for Port, months for Madeira and sweet Sherry
