What should I actually eat with Lebanese Cabernet Sauvignon?

Short Answer

Play to its strengths instead of fighting them. This is a wine built on ripe fruit and firm tannin, so it wants fat and char to soften it, not delicate flavours it'll flatten. Grilled lamb kafta or a lamb kebab is the obvious move, and it's obvious because it works: the char picks up the smoky, spiced notes in the wine, and the fat in the lamb coats your palate so the tannins feel silkier instead of gripping your tongue. Kibbeh, mujadara with a good crust, or even a simple grilled eggplant with tahini can handle it too, since the wine's herbal, savoury undertone actually echoes Levantine spicing rather than clashing with it. Avoid anything too light or delicate, like fish or a fresh salad, because the tannins will just steamroll it. Serve it around 17 to 18°C, not warm room temperature, or the alcohol and heat will feel more obvious than the fruit.
