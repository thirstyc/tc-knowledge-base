What pairs with Icewine?

Short Answer

The core rule with any dessert wine is that it needs to be at least as sweet as whatever's on the plate, otherwise the food makes the wine taste thin and sour by comparison. Icewine's sugar and acid are both extremely concentrated, so it can stand up to genuinely rich, sweet, or fatty foods without getting steamrolled. Classic pairings play on contrast: a wedge of blue cheese or foie gras against that syrupy sweetness works because the saltiness and fat cut through the sugar while the wine's acidity cuts back through the richness. On the dessert side, fruit tarts, crème brûlée, or anything with stone fruit echo its flavors rather than competing with them. Avoid pairing it with anything delicate or savory-dry, it will flatten the dish. Serve it cold, around 8 to 10 degrees, and in small pours, this is a wine meant to be sipped in ounces, not glasses.
