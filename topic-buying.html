Buying Wine

Good wine is less about spending more and more about knowing where to look. The best buys usually come from lesser-known regions, a shop that tastes what it sells, and a clear idea of what you like.

Best Value
Lesser-known regions and grapes, not famous names
Best Source
An independent shop or a knowledgeable store adviser
Save Money
Mixed-case discounts, bin ends, second wines
Watch For
Heat-damaged bottles, wrong vintages, prices that look too good
