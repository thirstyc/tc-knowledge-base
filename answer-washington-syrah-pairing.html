What pairs with Washington Syrah?

Short Answer

Washington State's Syrah, grown mostly in the Columbia Valley, gets intense sun during the day and cold nights, which pushes ripeness and dark fruit character while still preserving acidity, giving you a wine that's both powerful and structured rather than just jammy. That combination of firm tannin, dark fruit and a peppery, savory edge is what makes it such a strong match for grilled meats, the char and fat on the meat soften the tannin while the wine's acidity cuts through the richness. Lamb is a particularly good pairing, since its slightly gamey flavor stands up to the wine's intensity without being overpowered. The pepper and spice notes common in Washington Syrah also make it a surprisingly good partner for genuinely spicy food, the fruit sweetness and tannin structure act as a buffer against heat rather than amplifying it, unlike lighter, higher-acid reds. Serve it around 16-18°C, slightly cooler than room temperature, so the tannins stay firm and the wine doesn't taste hot or flabby.
