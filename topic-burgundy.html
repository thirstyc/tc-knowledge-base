Wine Region / France

Burgundy

The world's most detailed expression of terroir. Almost entirely single-variety — Pinot Noir for reds, Chardonnay for whites — with a fine-grained vineyard hierarchy that decides everything.

Key Grapes
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay
Climate
Cool continental
Known For
Terroir hierarchy, small fragmented vineyards
Homes
Chablis to the Mâconnais, eastern France
