Red Grape / Italy

Dolcetto

The easy-drinking Piedmont red. Low tannin, high acidity, fresh, fruity, cheap. Proof that something can be serious and fun at the same time. Drink it young, chill it slightly, pair it with everything.

Also Called
Dolcetto d'Alba, Dolcetto d'Asti
Typical Alcohol
11–13%
Tannin
Low-Medium
Homes
Piedmont (Italy)
