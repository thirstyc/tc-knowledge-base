White Grape / Italy

Moscato

Aromatic, often sweet. Low alcohol (5–10%), high sugar. Floral, honeyed. Asti Spumante's base. Fun, party wine, food-friendly. Proof that low alcohol and fun are not mutually exclusive.

Also Called
Muscat
Typical Alcohol
5–10%
Acidity
Low
Homes
Piedmont (Italy), Asti
