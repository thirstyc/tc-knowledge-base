Best Napa wine?

Topic

Cabernet Sauvignon

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

Napa's reputation rests on Cabernet Sauvignon, and its best wines come from a few celebrated sub-regions. It's expensive, so it pays to know where to look.

Key sub-regions for Cabernet:

  • Oakville and Rutherford: the classic valley-floor heart, rich and polished. Rutherford is known for a dusty, earthy note.
  • Stags Leap District: softer, more perfumed Cabernet.
  • Mountain areas like Howell Mountain, Spring Mountain and Mount Veeder: firmer, more tannic, age-worthy wines.
  • Coombsville and Oak Knoll: cooler, fresher styles, often better value.

Beyond Cabernet, Carneros, at Napa's cool southern end, makes excellent Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and sparkling wine. Napa Sauvignon Blanc is also very good and far cheaper than the reds.

Value strategies:

  • Look for second labels from top wineries.
  • Buy Napa Sauvignon Blanc or Chardonnay instead of Cabernet.
  • Try cooler sub-regions, which are often cheaper.
  • Look just outside Napa, to Sonoma's Alexander Valley or Lake County, for similar Cabernet for less.

Serious Napa Cabernet ages well, often for 15–20 years or more, and young bottles benefit from decanting.
