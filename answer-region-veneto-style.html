Why does Veneto wine taste so different bottle to bottle?

Short Answer

One region making everything from bone-dry Soave to sparkling Prosecco to concentrated, dried-grape Amarone. Corvina reds range from light Valpolicella to intense, raisinated power. Range is the whole identity here.

Why is Veneto Italy's most commercially important wine region?

More wine by volume than any other Italian region, and it still produces serious, age-worthy bottles like Amarone.
