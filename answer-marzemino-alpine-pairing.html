What pairs with Marzemino?

Short Answer

Marzemino is light-bodied with soft tannins and real acidity, so it wants food that won't bully it. Grilled trout or other freshwater fish work because the wine has enough backbone to handle a bit of char without the tannins clashing against delicate flesh. Light meats like veal or chicken, especially prepared simply, let its berry fruit and faint herbal note come through instead of getting buried. Fresh pasta with a light tomato or herb sauce is a natural match too, since the wine's brightness plays well with acidity on the plate rather than competing against heavy cream or oil. This is Alpine table wine at heart, built for the kind of unfussy, mountain cooking you'd find in Trentino: cured meats, soft cheeses, simple vegetable dishes. Serve it slightly cool, around 14-16°C, cooler than a typical red but not cold, so the fruit stays fresh and the tannins don't feel rougher than they are.

What pairs with Marsanne?

Marsanne's waxy texture and low acidity mean it doesn't cut through fat the way a Sauvignon Blanc would, so pair it with dishes where you want the wine to match richness rather than slice through it.
