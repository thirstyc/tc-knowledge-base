How Wine Works

Tannins

The compounds that make your mouth feel dry after a big red. They come from grape skins, seeds, stems, and oak, and they're the reason some wines can age for decades.

Comes From
Skins, seeds, stems, oak barrels
Feels Like
Drying, gripping sensation on the palate
Softens With
Time in bottle, decanting, fatty food
Found Most In
Cabernet Sauvignon, Nebbiolo, Sagrantino
