Best wine for spring?

Short Answer

Fresh, bright and green-leaning: Sauvignon Blanc, Grüner Veltliner, dry rosé and light reds. Spring food is lighter, full of new vegetables and herbs, and the wines should follow.

Sauvignon Blanc and Grüner Veltliner are ideal with spring vegetables like asparagus, peas and artichokes, which are notoriously hard to pair. Their green, herbal notes echo the food.

Dry Riesling, Albariño and Vermentino suit seafood and salads.

Dry rosé comes back out as the weather warms, and it's great with spring lunches.

Light reds, like Beaujolais, Pinot Noir and Gamay, work well with lamb, a spring classic, and are best served slightly chilled.

Spring food pairings:

  • Asparagus with Sauvignon Blanc or Grüner Veltliner.
  • Roast spring lamb with Pinot Noir, Rioja or Bordeaux.
  • Pea and mint dishes with Sauvignon Blanc.
  • Goat cheese salads with Sancerre.
  • Brunch with sparkling wine.

Spring is also when many producers release their fresh whites and rosés from the previous harvest, so it's a good time to try new vintages.
