Why is Etna Sicily's most exciting wine story?

Short Answer

A volcanic frontier that top winemakers from all over Italy have flocked to in the last two decades. Old vines, extreme altitude, prices climbing fast. Sicily went from bulk wine island to cult terroir almost overnight.
