Wine with BBQ sauce?

Topic

Grenache

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

A ripe, juicy red with soft tannins, like Zinfandel, Shiraz, Malbec or Grenache. BBQ sauce is sweet, smoky and tangy, and it needs a wine with enough fruit to match that sweetness.

Zinfandel is the classic choice. Its jammy fruit and peppery spice pair beautifully with ribs and pulled pork.

Australian Shiraz and Argentine Malbec also work well, especially with beef.

The sauce style matters.

Sweet, thick sauces, like Kansas City style, pair best with very fruit-forward reds like Zinfandel and Shiraz.

Vinegar-based sauces, like Carolina style, are tangy and lighter. A juicy Beaujolais, a dry rosé or an off-dry Riesling handles the acidity well.

Texas-style brisket, often served with little sauce and lots of smoke, can take a more structured red like Syrah, Malbec or Cabernet Sauvignon.

Spicy BBQ sauces call for lower alcohol and some sweetness, so an off-dry Riesling or a fruity rosé is a smart pick.

Alabama white sauce, made with mayonnaise and vinegar, suits a crisp white like Sauvignon Blanc or an unoaked Chardonnay with chicken.

What to avoid: austere, very dry or delicate wines. The sweet sauce makes them taste thin and sour.
