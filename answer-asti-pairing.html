What pairs with Asti?

Short Answer

Asti's whole personality is low alcohol (usually 5-7%), noticeable sweetness, and a light, gentle fizz from the tank method, so you want food that either matches or plays against that sweetness rather than fighting it. Fruit-based desserts, panna cotta, light cakes, and biscotti work because the wine's sugar mirrors the dish instead of getting steamrolled by it. It also does something clever with spicy food: the sweetness and low alcohol cool down chili heat instead of amplifying it the way a big dry red would, so it's a genuinely good match for Thai or Sichuan dishes. Skip pairing it with anything rich, fatty, or heavily savoury, like steak or aged cheese, since the wine has neither the tannin nor the alcohol to stand up to that weight. Serve it well chilled, around 6-8°C, and drink it young. This isn't a wine that improves with age or with an overly serious dinner plan, it's built for brunch, dessert, or an easy afternoon.
