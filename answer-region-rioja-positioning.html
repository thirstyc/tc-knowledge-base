Why is Rioja Spain's most recognizable wine name?

Short Answer

Aging categories — Crianza, Reserva, Gran Reserva — tell you exactly how much oak and bottle time you're getting. Reliable, graded, easy to buy with confidence. The name most people say when they think "Spanish red."
