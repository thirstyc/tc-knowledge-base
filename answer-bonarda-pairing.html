What pairs with Bonarda?

Short Answer

Because Bonarda leans on juicy acidity rather than tannin for structure, it doesn't need fatty, protein-heavy food the way a tannic red does, it just needs something with a bit of acid or freshness of its own to match. Grilled chicken works because the char adds savoury weight without overwhelming the wine's soft fruit. Fresh pasta with tomato or herb-based sauces is a natural fit since the acid in both plays well together. Even lighter fare like salads with a vinaigrette can work, which is unusual for a red, because Bonarda doesn't have the tannin that normally clashes with vinegar. Empanadas and other Argentine street food are the regional pairing for a reason, casual, a little fatty, no fuss. Serve it around 14-16°C rather than full room temperature, since a slight chill keeps the fruit bright and stops the wine from feeling flat. This is a bottle for a weeknight table, not a special-occasion roast.
