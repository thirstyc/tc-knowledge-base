White Grape / France

Muscadet

Light, crisp, mineral. Low alcohol, high acidity. Loire Valley's most affordable white. Sur lie aging adds texture. Proof that affordable and serious aren't opposites. The oyster wine.

Also Called
Melon de Bourgogne, Muscadet Sèvre et Maine
Typical Alcohol
11–12%
Acidity
Very High
Homes
Loire Valley (France), Nantes
