What pairs with Roussanne?

Short Answer

Roussanne carries real weight on the palate, waxy, textured, with notes of honeysuckle, herbal tea and sometimes a faint nuttiness, and only moderate acidity to keep it in check. That combination is exactly why it suits roasted white fish and cream-based sauces: the wine's body matches the richness of the dish instead of getting overwhelmed by it, while what acid it has keeps the pairing from feeling heavy or flat. The same logic applies to soft, bloomy-rind cheeses, where the wine's texture mirrors the cheese's creaminess rather than cutting against it. This isn't a wine for squeezing lemon over grilled fish or pairing with anything sharp and acidic; it wants richness to lean into, not contrast against. Serve it around 12 to 14°C, a touch warmer than a lean Sauvignon Blanc, so the aromatics and texture can actually show. It's a Rhône white built for indulgence, not refreshment, and food should follow that lead.
