How Wine Works

Natural Wine

Natural wine is made from grapes farmed without synthetic chemicals, fermented with the wild yeasts already on their skins, with nothing added or taken away in the cellar except, sometimes, a little sulfur. That's it. What it isn't is a regulated category: in most countries the term has no legal definition, so it describes a producer's intentions, not a guarantee. The best bottles are vivid and alive. The worst are faulty wine with a good story.

What It Is
Grapes farmed without synthetic chemicals, wild yeast, no fining or filtering
What It Isn't
A quality guarantee, or a regulated term in most countries
Closest To A Rulebook
France’s "Vin Méthode Nature" label, since 2020
Common In
Beaujolais, Loire Valley, Languedoc, Jura
