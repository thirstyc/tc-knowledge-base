How Wine Works

Natural Wine

Minimal intervention taken as far as a producer dares: grapes grown without synthetic chemicals, fermented with the wild yeasts already on their skins, and very little added or taken away in the cellar. No single legal definition applies everywhere, so the term covers everything from vivid and alive to genuinely faulty. Taste first, judge later.

No Legal Definition
France’s "Vin Méthode Nature" label is the closest thing
Usually Means
No fining, no filtration, little or no added sulfites
In The Glass
Cloudiness, a bit of fizz, juicy fruit, sometimes funk
Common In
Beaujolais, Loire Valley, Languedoc, Jura
