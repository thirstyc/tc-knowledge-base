Why Does Assyrtiko Taste Like You're Standing on a Cliff?

Topic

Topics

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

Terroir, mainly. Assyrtiko grows on volcanic rock in Santorini. The soil is harsh, minerals are intense, and the vines are old. High acidity + mineral richness = you literally taste the island. The salinity isn't salt, it's mineral extraction. High alcohol (13–15%) makes it taste like sun and heat. Pair it with oysters, sea urchin, raw fish. The wine tastes like seaside.
