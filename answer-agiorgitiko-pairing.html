What pairs with Agiorgitiko?

Short Answer

Agiorgitiko (St. George) is Greece's most-planted red, centered in Nemea in the Peloponnese, and it's actually gentler than its reputation suggests—medium tannin, juicy red cherry and plum, a savory herbal streak from warm, high-altitude vineyards. Don't picture something gripping like Nebbiolo; picture a spiced, fruit-forward Merlot. That softer structure is what makes it so food-friendly. Grilled lamb with oregano and lemon is the classic Peloponnese match: the wine's acid cuts the fat, and since the tannin is mild it doesn't fight the char. Moussaka works for the same reason—tomato and cinnamon-laced meat need enough acidity to reset the palate, but too much tannin would clash with the béchamel. It's equally good with grilled sausages, roasted eggplant, or aged kefalotyri. Serve around 16-17°C, a touch cooler than you'd pour a heavy Cabernet, to keep the fruit bright rather than jammy. What it doesn't want is delicate fish or anything sweet—this is a wine that needs some fat or smoke on the plate to show its best side.
