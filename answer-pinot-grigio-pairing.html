What pairs with Pinot Grigio?

Short Answer

Pinot Grigio's whole appeal is what it doesn't do: low aromatic intensity, moderate acidity, light body, nothing that demands attention or fights with delicate food. That makes it genuinely useful with things that a more assertive white would flatten, like a simple green salad with vinaigrette, raw or lightly cooked shellfish, fresh mozzarella, or a plain seafood pasta without heavy sauce. It's not a wine to reach for with anything rich, spiced, or aged, since it has no acid backbone or fruit weight to back it up, it'll just vanish. Serve it cold, around 8-10°C, which suits its lack of complexity, there's nothing to warm up and reveal. Think of it less as a wine you pair deliberately and more as a wine that gets out of the way, which on a hot day with simple food is genuinely the point, not a failure of ambition.
