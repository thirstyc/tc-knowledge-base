What pairs with Chardonnay?

Short Answer

Rich fish (halibut). Roasted chicken. Cream sauce. Lobster. The full body handles luxury food. It's the fine-dining white.

Can Chardonnay age?

Yes, the best Chardonnay can age for ten to twenty years or more, but most is made to drink within a few years of release.
