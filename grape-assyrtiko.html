White Grape / Greece

Assyrtiko

Mineral, saline, high-acid. Santorini's flagship white. Volcanic terroir in a glass. High alcohol, crisp finish. The wine that tastes like eating sea minerals while standing on a cliff.

Also Called

Typical Alcohol
13–15%
Acidity
Very High
Homes
Santorini (Greece)
