Riesling

Aromatic, mineral, can be dry or sweet. High acidity, low alcohol. The most misunderstood grape—not all Riesling is sweet, and that's the whole point. Terroir translates here better than anywhere.

Also Called
Johannisberg Riesling
Typical Alcohol
10–13%
Acidity
High
Homes
Alsace (France), Mosel (Germany), Austria
