What pairs with California Chardonnay?

Short Answer

Match the wine's weight and its buttery, oak-driven notes rather than treating it like a generic white. Lobster or crab with drawn butter is the classic pairing because the wine's own butter-like diacetyl notes echo the dish instead of fighting it. Roast chicken or turkey with pan gravy works for the same reason, richness meeting richness. Cream-based sauces, risotto, or scallops seared in butter all lean the same direction, and the wine's oak-derived vanilla and toast notes hold up well against roasted or caramelized flavours, like grilled corn or roasted root vegetables. Where it struggles is with very delicate raw fish, sushi, or anything acidic and bright like a squeeze-of-lemon salad, the oak and lower acidity just overwhelm those dishes. Serve it around 10-12°C, slightly warmer than a lean white, so the oak and texture show rather than getting muted by cold. If you land a leaner, unoaked California Chardonnay instead, treat it more like a Chablis and go lighter, with oysters or simple grilled fish.
