Is Assyrtiko Only From Santorini?

Short Answer

The grape itself isn't exclusive to Santorini, you'll find plantings in Paros, on the Greek mainland around Drama, and in a handful of other spots, but the overwhelming majority of what reaches export markets, and virtually all of what people mean when they rave about Assyrtiko, is Santorini fruit. The island's volcanic ash and pumice soils, near-total lack of rainfall, and constant wind stress the vines hard, which concentrates flavor and pushes acidity and salinity to levels other regions simply don't replicate, even growing the same grape. Assyrtiko from Paros or the mainland tends to be softer, rounder, less taut, pleasant but missing that electric, saline intensity. If the label says Santorini PDO, the wine legally has to come from grapes grown on the island under its specific rules, so that's your guarantee of the real thing. Everywhere else, it's the same grape wearing a different, gentler personality, worth trying but not a substitute for the original.
