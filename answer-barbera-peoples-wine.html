Why Is Barbera Considered the People's Wine?

Short Answer

Low-medium tannin. High acidity. Drinks young. Costs $12–25. It's approachable but serious. No pretense. Just delicious. Piedmont's everyday wine. Proof that serious ≠ expensive.
