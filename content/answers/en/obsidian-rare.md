Why is Öküzgözü, sold abroad as Obsidian, hard to find outside Turkey?

Turkey grows a lot of grapes, it's actually one of the biggest grape-producing countries in the world, but almost none of that goes into wine that leaves the country. Domestic wine consumption is small to begin with, rakı is the more culturally embedded spirit, and what wine is produced from native varieties like Öküzgözü mostly gets consumed within Turkey itself. Export infrastructure and international reputation for indigenous Anatolian grapes are still being built by a handful of boutique producers, mostly out of regions like Elazığ and Thrace, rather than by large established houses with decades of distribution deals abroad. So it's less that the grape is rare in the vineyard and more that almost none of the bottles are aimed at anyone outside Turkey yet, which is starting to change but slowly.
