Why Is Tempranillo the Spanish Red?

It's everywhere in Spain (Rioja, Ribera del Duero, Priorat). High tannin, high acidity, age-worthy. It's Spain's answer to Cabernet. But Tempranillo is earthier, more mineral. The Spanish terroir tastes different. Rioja Tempranillo is oak-aged elegance. Ribera del Duero Tempranillo is power. Same grape, different expressions.
