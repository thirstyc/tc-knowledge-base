What's the Difference Between Tokaji Sweet and Dry Furmint?

The grapes are the same. Tokaji sweet uses noble rot (botrytis) + oxidative aging. Concentrated, golden, 50–150g residual sugar. Expensive, complex, rare. Dry Furmint is made fully dry (0g sugar), high acidity, mineral. Totally different wines, same grape. Dry Furmint is the sleeper hit. Fraction of the price, serious wine. Tokaji is dessert; dry Furmint is dinner.
