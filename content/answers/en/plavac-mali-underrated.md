Why does Plavac Mali fly under the radar?

Croatia's wine industry got flattened by war and decades of collectivized, cooperative-style production before that, and it's still clawing back the export infrastructure and reputation that countries like Italy or Spain never lost. The DNA link to Zinfandel, confirmed by researchers tracing it back to an old Croatian variety called Crljenak Kaštelanski, is a great story that just hasn't reached casual drinkers yet. Labeling doesn't help either: top subzones like Dingač and Postup are basically unknown outside Croatia, so a serious, cellar-worthy bottle looks the same on a shelf as something forgettable. The result is a weird pricing paradox, wines with the concentration and ambition of cult Zinfandel or Amarone, going for a fraction of the price because nobody's fighting over them yet. That's arguably the best reason to buy it now rather than a reason to skip it.
