What pairs with Jura Vin Jaune or Savagnin?

Vin Jaune and oxidative Savagnin are made under a veil of flor yeast, similar to sherry, which strips out fresh fruit character and replaces it with walnut, curry spice, and a dry, almost saline tang. That's a hard flavour profile to match with anything delicate, which is why the pairings that work are all things that share those same nutty, savoury notes rather than fighting them. Comté, especially aged Comté with some crystalline crunch, has the same walnut-and-caramel thing going on from its own long affinage, so the wine and cheese just reinforce each other. Roast chicken with a cream sauce gives you richness and fat that the wine's acidity and oxidative tang can cut through without clashing. Morels, with their earthy, slightly nutty depth, echo the wine directly rather than contrasting with it. This is about as regional a pairing as wine gets: Jura food and Jura wine were built around each other for centuries, so straying too far outside that pantry rarely works as well.
