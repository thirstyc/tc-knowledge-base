Why does Hawke's Bay Merlot taste so ripe yet structured?

New Zealand's warmest major region, gravelly Gimblett Gravels soil that drains fast and stresses the vines. Riper fruit than Bordeaux, still real structure underneath. New Zealand's answer to a Bordeaux blend.
