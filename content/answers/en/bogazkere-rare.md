Why is Boğazkere hard to find outside Turkey?

Turkey is actually one of the oldest wine-producing regions on earth, with vines going back thousands of years in Anatolia, so the scarcity isn't about lack of wine, it's about lack of export infrastructure. Domestic consumption is low relative to production because of the country's demographics, and the industry has historically focused on selling to Turkish drinkers rather than building distribution networks abroad. Boğazkere specifically is a native grape grown mainly around Diyarbakır in the southeast, a region with little tradition of shipping wine internationally compared to established European exporters. Add in inconsistent quality standards until relatively recently and limited marketing abroad, and you get a grape that's genuinely respected at home but nearly invisible on foreign shelves. That's changing slowly as a handful of quality-focused Turkish producers start targeting export markets, but for now finding a bottle outside Turkey usually means a specialist shop or a restaurant with a genuinely adventurous list.
