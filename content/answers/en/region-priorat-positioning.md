Why did Priorat go from forgotten to elite?

Nearly abandoned by the 1970s, revived by a handful of winemakers chasing old vines and impossible terrain. Now one of only two Spanish regions with the top DOCa classification, alongside Rioja. Proof a dying region can become a cult one.
