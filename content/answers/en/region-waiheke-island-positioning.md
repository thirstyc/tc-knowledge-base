Why is Waiheke Island such a novelty in wine circles?

Tiny production, boutique wineries, some of New Zealand's highest prices per bottle. A short ferry ride from Auckland, built as much for wine tourism as for the wine itself. Proof that great terroir can hide in the smallest places.
