What pairs with Corvina?

The low tannin is the whole game here. Tannin is what makes red wine clash with delicate food, so without much of it Corvina doesn't fight anything, it just sits alongside. Grilled fish and roasted poultry work because there's no bitter grip to overpower them, and the acidity does the job tannin usually does with fattier foods, cutting through olive oil and butter instead of getting bulldozed by them. Fresh pasta with tomato sauce is close to a natural match since the cherry fruit in the wine echoes the acidity of the tomatoes rather than competing with it. Light salads work for the same reason, nothing in the wine is heavy enough to overwhelm greens or vinaigrette. Where it falls apart is anything with real richness or char, a steak or aged hard cheese, because there's no tannic backbone to stand up to that. Keep it simple, keep it fresh, serve it a touch cool at 14-16°C, and let the wine be the quiet one at the table.
