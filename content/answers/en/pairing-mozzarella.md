Wine with mozzarella?

A crisp, fresh white like Pinot Grigio, Vermentino or Falanghina, or a dry rosé. Mozzarella is mild, milky and delicate, so the wine should be light and refreshing, not overpowering.

Italian whites from the south are the natural match. Falanghina and Fiano come from Campania, the home of mozzarella di bufala, and their citrus and floral notes suit it perfectly. Greco di Tufo and Vermentino work too.

Caprese salad, with tomato, basil and olive oil, needs a wine with enough acidity for the tomato: Sauvignon Blanc, Vermentino or a dry rosé.

Burrata, richer and creamier, can take a white with a little more texture, like a Soave, a lightly oaked Chardonnay or Champagne.

Pizza with mozzarella and tomato sauce shifts the pairing toward light reds with good acidity: Barbera, Chianti, Montepulciano d'Abruzzo or a chilled Lambrusco.

Fried mozzarella and mozzarella sticks love sparkling wine, whose bubbles cut through the fried coating.

What to avoid: heavy, oaky whites and tannic reds. They overwhelm the cheese's gentle flavour.
