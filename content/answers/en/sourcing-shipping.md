Can I have wine shipped to me?

Often yes, but it depends entirely on where you live. Alcohol shipping is heavily regulated, and the rules differ by country, and often by province or state.

Some examples of how different it can be:

- In Canada, each province controls alcohol sales, often through a government retailer. Rules on direct delivery from wineries and other provinces vary, so check your province's current regulations.
- In the United States, each state sets its own rules. Many allow direct shipping from wineries with limits, while shipping from out-of-state retailers is more restricted.
- Within the European Union, buying from other member states is generally possible, but excise duties and taxes apply.
- Some countries restrict alcohol imports or delivery heavily.

Practical points wherever you are:

- Buy from licensed retailers and wineries that ship legally to your area. They'll usually tell you at checkout.
- Expect an adult signature and ID check on delivery.
- Watch the weather, since heat and freezing can damage wine in transit.
- For international shipments, factor in duty, taxes and possible delays.

If you're unsure, your local liquor authority or a licensed retailer can explain what's allowed where you live.
