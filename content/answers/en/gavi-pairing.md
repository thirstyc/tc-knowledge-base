What pairs with Gavi?

Gavi's whole personality is acid and restraint, which makes it a scalpel rather than a blanket at the table. That sharp acidity cuts through the natural richness of raw fish and shellfish without fighting them, and it resets your palate between bites instead of layering more fat on top. Raw oysters, crudo, carpaccio of fish, and simple seafood pasta with olive oil and lemon are the classic matches because there's no competing sauce weight for the wine to wrestle. It also works well with fried calamari or light fritto misto, where the acidity slices through the oil the same way a squeeze of lemon would. Avoid anything creamy, oaked, or built around red meat or heavy tomato reduction, the wine will just taste thin and irrelevant next to it. Serve it properly cold, 8 to 10°C, so that acid stays taut instead of turning flabby as it warms in the glass.
