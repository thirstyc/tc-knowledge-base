Why does Hawke's Bay matter beyond Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc?

It proved New Zealand could make serious red wine, not just zesty whites. Gimblett Gravels is one of the country's most respected single terroirs. The quiet, ambitious side of New Zealand wine.
