Why Is Dry Furmint So Mineral and Age-Worthy?

Volcanic soils in Tokaji region (Northeast Hungary). High acidity (think: Riesling levels). Ancient vines. The combination = mineral extraction + acidity preservation. Furmint aged 10+ years develops honey, complexity, depth. It's like Riesling but earthier. The acidity keeps it fresh. Mineral persists. It's seriously age-worthy. But nobody ages it because it costs $12–20 retail. The value is insane.
