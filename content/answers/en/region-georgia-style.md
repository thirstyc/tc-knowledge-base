Why does Georgian wine taste so tannic and earthy, even the whites?

Skin-contact "orange" wine fermented in buried clay vessels called qvevri, a method practiced for 8,000 years. Whites pick up tannin and texture usually found only in reds. The origin point of wine itself, still making it the same way.
