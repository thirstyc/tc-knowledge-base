Why does Piedmont Nebbiolo taste so tannic and floral?

Thin-skinned but ferociously tannic grape, fog-cooled hills — the name literally means "little fogs." Rose petal and tar on the nose, serious structure underneath. Deceptively pale, deceptively powerful.
