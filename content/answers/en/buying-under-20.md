Best wine under $20?

You can find excellent wine under $20 if you skip the famous names and look for regions and grapes known for value. Prices vary a lot by country and tax, but the same places tend to deliver.

For reds, look for:

- Côtes du Rhône and other southern French blends.
- Malbec from Mendoza.
- Portuguese reds from the Douro or Alentejo.
- Montepulciano d'Abruzzo and Nero d'Avola.
- Beaujolais-Villages.
- Spanish Garnacha and Monastrell.
- Chilean Carmenère.

For whites, look for:

- Muscadet, Picpoul de Pinet and Côtes de Gascogne.
- Vinho Verde.
- Verdejo from Rueda.
- South African Chenin Blanc.
- Sauvignon Blanc from Chile or New Zealand.

For sparkling, Cava, Prosecco and many Crémants fit the budget.

What to be careful with: the cheapest bottles from famous, expensive regions. Budget Champagne, Burgundy and Bordeaux often disappoint, because you're paying for the name rather than the quality.

A few tips:

- The difference between a $10 and a $20 bottle is often bigger than between $20 and $40.
- Ask shop staff for their favourite under your budget. They taste a lot.
- Screwcaps are not a sign of cheap wine.
