What pairs with Vintage Port?

Stilton and Vintage Port is the pairing people actually build cellars around, and it works because contrast, not harmony, is the mechanism: the wine's sugar and grip cut straight through the cheese's salt and fat, while the blue mold's savory funk tempers the fruit so it doesn't taste cloying. Walnuts do something similar, their bitterness and oil balancing the sweetness. Dark chocolate works for the same reason. Where it falls apart is with anything delicate, fruit tarts, light desserts, custards, since the wine's tannin and 20-ish percent alcohol just flatten them. Decant before serving; Vintage Port is bottled unfiltered and throws heavy sediment as it ages, and pouring it straight means a gritty glass. Serve around 16 to 18°C, properly cool cellar temperature, never warm room temperature, or the alcohol dominates.
