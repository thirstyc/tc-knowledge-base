Best wine for a date night?

Start with something sparkling, then share a versatile red like Pinot Noir or a Beaujolais cru. Bubbles set the mood, and a food-friendly red won't fight whatever you order.

Sparkling wine is the easiest opener. Champagne if you want to make a point, but a Crémant from France, a Spanish Cava or an English sparkling wine can be just as good for much less.

For the main bottle, choose something that works with lots of dishes, since you'll probably order different things. Pinot Noir is light enough for fish and substantial enough for duck or steak. Beaujolais crus like Morgon or Fleurie are juicy, fun and reasonably priced. If you both prefer white, a Chablis or a dry Riesling covers a similar range.

Keep an eye on the alcohol. A 15% red can make the evening feel heavy fast, while wines around 12–13% are easier to enjoy over a long dinner.

Ask the sommelier. Telling them your budget and what you like is not a weakness; it's the fastest way to find something great. And ordering by the glass lets you try more than one wine.

At home, pick something you already know you both like, chill it properly, and don't overthink it.

The best date-night wine is the one that lets you pay attention to each other, not the label.
