Why does Campania deserve more attention?

Aglianico is called the "Barolo of the south" for a reason, and Fiano and Greco are among Italy's most age-worthy whites. Ancient Greek and Roman vineyards, still farmed today. Overshadowed by Tuscany and Piedmont, unfairly.
