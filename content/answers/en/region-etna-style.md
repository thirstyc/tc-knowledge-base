Why does Etna wine taste so smoky and volcanic?

Vines planted in black volcanic ash on the slopes of an active volcano, high altitude keeping acid sharp. Nerello Mascalese reds land somewhere between Pinot Noir and Nebbiolo. Terroir you can practically taste erupting.
