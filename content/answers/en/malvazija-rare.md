Why is Malvazija hard to find outside Croatia?

Malvazija Istarska is Istria's signature white, grown almost exclusively along Croatia's northern Adriatic coast, and Croatia's wine industry simply hasn't built the export infrastructure that regions like Burgundy or Rioja have had decades to establish. Production is dominated by small, family-run wineries rather than large export-focused producers, and much of what's made gets consumed domestically or by the steady stream of tourists who visit Istria each summer and drink it on the spot. There's also been little incentive to chase international markets when local demand already absorbs most of the supply. That's slowly changing as a handful of ambitious Istrian producers start courting sommeliers and importers abroad, but the wine still remains something of an insider's pick outside Croatia. If you do find a bottle, it's worth grabbing, since it offers a distinctive, saline, mineral-driven style you won't easily replicate elsewhere. Serve it well chilled, around 8 to 10°C, to keep its bright acidity front and center.
