What pairs with Ribolla Gialla?

Ribolla Gialla comes from Friuli-Venezia Giulia, particularly Collio and Colli Orientali, right against the Slovenian border where the grape is also known as Rebula. It's made two ways: a clean, stainless-steel version with citrus and green apple, or a skin-contact "orange wine" style with tannin and texture from extended maceration. The clean, acid-driven style loves shellfish, oysters, and fresh pasta with olive oil or lemon - the acidity cuts through richness and resets your palate between bites. The skin-contact style, with its tannic grip and nutty, dried-apricot notes, actually handles heavier food better: aged cheese, cured meats, even mushroom dishes. Serve the clean style around 10-12°C, the orange wine style a touch warmer, 12-14°C, so you don't mute its texture and phenolic complexity. Don't ice it down like a generic pinot grigio - that kills the character that makes Ribolla worth seeking out in the first place.
