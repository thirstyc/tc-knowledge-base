What pairs with Vermentino?

Vermentino grows along the Mediterranean coast, in Sardinia, Liguria and parts of Tuscany, and it tastes like it: briny, herbal, with a saline edge and just enough acidity to feel refreshing without being sharp. That coastal character is why it pairs so naturally with seafood, particularly simply prepared fish, shellfish and anything with a squeeze of lemon, the wine essentially continues the flavor profile of the dish rather than contrasting it. Light salads and fresh pasta benefit from the same acid-driven freshness, while soft, young cheeses get a lift from the wine's slight herbal bitterness on the finish. It's genuinely earned its reputation as a beach wine, low in alcohol, easy to drink, and forgiving of being served a bit too cold on a hot day. Serve it around 8-10°C. Don't overthink the pairing here, Vermentino is not a wine that wants a complicated tasting menu, it wants grilled fish, a view of the water, and not much else.
