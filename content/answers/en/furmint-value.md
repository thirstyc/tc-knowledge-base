Why Is Furmint the Best Value White in Eastern Europe?

Because Eastern Europe isn't fashionable. Burgundy Chardonnay costs $40+. Austrian Grüner Veltliner costs $30+. Dry Furmint costs $12–20 and tastes as serious. High acidity, mineral intensity, age potential. The only barrier is: people don't know it. Marketing, not quality. If Furmint had a Napa label, it would cost 3x as much. It's the wine insiders order.
