Best wine for mushroom risotto?

Pinot Noir or a Piedmont red like Barbera or Nebbiolo. Mushrooms are earthy and savoury, and these wines share those flavours without overwhelming the creamy rice.

Pinot Noir is the classic choice. Its red fruit and forest-floor notes, especially in red Burgundy, sit perfectly alongside mushrooms.

Mushroom risotto is at home in northern Italy, so Piedmont wines make sense too. Barbera is juicy and fresh, with enough acidity to cut through the butter and Parmesan. Langhe Nebbiolo adds more structure.

If there's truffle, reach for Nebbiolo, ideally a Barolo or Barbaresco with some age. Truffle and aged Nebbiolo are a famous combination.

Prefer white? Pick one with some body and texture, like an oaked Chardonnay or white Burgundy, which matches the creaminess.

For a more adventurous pairing, try a dry Sherry like Fino or Amontillado. Its nutty, savoury flavour and umami echo the mushrooms.

What to avoid: very tannic reds like young Cabernet Sauvignon. Mushrooms are rich in umami, which makes tannin taste harsher and more bitter.
