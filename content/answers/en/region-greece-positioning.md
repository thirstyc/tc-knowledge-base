Why is Greece finally getting taken seriously for wine?

Thousands of years of winemaking history, buried under decades of retsina's bad reputation. Assyrtiko and Agiorgitiko are proving old vines and volcanic soil beat mass-market stereotypes. The most exciting comeback story in wine.
