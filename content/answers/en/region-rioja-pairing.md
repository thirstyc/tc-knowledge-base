What pairs with Rioja?

Rioja was basically built around a Spanish table, so it pairs the way a longtime couple finishes each other's sentences. Grilled chorizo works because the wine's dried red fruit and gentle spice from oak aging mirror the smoky paprika fat of the sausage without either one shouting over the other. Lamb chops, especially char-grilled, need a wine with enough tannin to cut the fat and enough savoriness to match the char, and a Reserva-level Rioja with a few years of oak does exactly that. Manchego, particularly an aged one with some crystalline crunch, plays off the wine's dried fruit and vanilla the same way a good cheese plays off any oaked red, but the specific saltiness and nutty depth of Manchego was practically made for this style. None of this is exotic pairing theory, it's just what happens when a wine and a cuisine develop side by side for centuries. Serve it around 16 to 18°C, not fridge cold, so the tannin and fruit stay in balance instead of turning hard.
