Why Does Cabernet Sauvignon Age So Well?

High tannin. High acidity. High structure. Those three = longevity. The tannins soften over decades. Acidity keeps it fresh. The fruit fades, but secondary flavors (leather, tobacco, earth) emerge. A 20-year Cabernet tastes different than year one—better or worse depends on you. But it won't spoil. The structure protects it. That's why Cabernet costs more young.
