Wine for Mother's Day?

Something that feels like a treat and suits a spring brunch or lunch: Champagne or another good sparkling wine, a dry rosé, or a bottle from a region she loves.

Sparkling wine is the natural choice for a celebration, and it pairs with almost any brunch dish, including eggs, which trip up most wines. A Crémant or English sparkling wine feels special without costing as much as Champagne.

Dry rosé suits spring lunches, salads and seafood, and it's easy to enjoy.

For a gift, personal beats expensive:

- A wine from a place she's visited or wants to visit.
- A bottle she enjoyed once and mentioned.
- A half bottle of a dessert wine like Sauternes or Moscato d'Asti, paired with her favourite dessert.
- A wine with a note about why you chose it.

If you're cooking, match the wine to the meal: sparkling wine for brunch, Pinot Noir for salmon or lamb, a crisp white for fish.

If she doesn't drink much, a good non-alcoholic sparkling wine or a single glass of something special can mean more than a full bottle.
