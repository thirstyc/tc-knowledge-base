Can You Age Dry Assyrtiko?

Should You?

Yes. Most Assyrtiko is drunk young (1–2 years). But 5–8 year bottles develop honey, oxidative notes, deeper complexity. Not Sauternes-level evolution, but noticeable. The high acidity preserves the wine. Will it taste better aged? Debatable. Aged Assyrtiko is winery expression, not a necessity. Drink it however you want. Young = crisp, bracing, pure. Aged = deeper, honeyed, mineral. Both work.
