What pairs with Kerner?

Kerner's combination of moderate acidity, light-to-medium body and a floral, slightly off-dry profile makes it good at handling food that would clash with something drier and sharper. Light seafood - shrimp, scallops, white fish in a butter or cream sauce - works because the wine's residual sugar buffers any richness without fighting it. Fresh pasta with a light cream or herb sauce does the same job. Soft, young cheeses (think fresh goat cheese or a mild brie) play well too, since the wine's floral lift cuts through the fat while its sweetness smooths out any tang. Avoid pairing it with anything too spicy or too rich - the wine doesn't have the acid backbone or tannin to stand up to heavy, fatty dishes, and it will just taste flabby next to them. Serve at 8-10°C. Think of Kerner as a wine for lighter spring and summer meals rather than a dinner-table workhorse.
