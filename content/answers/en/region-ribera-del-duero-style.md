Why does Ribera del Duero taste so much darker and denser than Rioja?

Higher altitude, more extreme temperature swings, thicker-skinned Tempranillo clones. Less oak-driven vanilla, more raw concentration and dark fruit. Same grape as Rioja, completely different attitude.
