What pairs with Arinto?

Arinto's whole personality is acid and salinity, so it wants food that's rich, oily, or briny enough to be cut through. Grilled sardines, salt cod, other Portuguese seafood staples are the obvious match because that's literally what the wine grew up next to. Shellfish — clams, mussels, anything with a squeeze of lemon already on it — works for the same reason. Light pasta with olive oil and herbs is fine, but avoid anything creamy or heavily buttered; the wine's job is to slice through fat, and a sauce that competes on richness just fights it instead of playing along. Fresh salads with a vinaigrette are safe too, since the wine's acid is already higher than most dressings. Serve it well chilled, 8–10°C, so that mineral snap stays tight. What you don't want is red meat or anything smoky and charred — Arinto has no tannin or weight to back that up.
