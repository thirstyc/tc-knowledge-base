Can You Drink Cabernet Young or Does It Need Age?

You can. Young Cabernet is grippy (high tannin), tight. Aggressive. Not pleasant. But drinkable. Age it 5+ years and it opens up. The tannins soften. It tastes better. Should you age it? If you paid $100+, yes. If you paid $15, drink it now. High-end Cabernet benefits from age. Budget Cabernet is ready now.
