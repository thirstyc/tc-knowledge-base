What pairs with Stellenbosch Cabernet or Pinotage?

These wines are built around tannin and fruit density strong enough to survive direct flame, so pair them with food that's been through the same fire. Braai chars proteins and triggers Maillard browning, producing roasted, savoury compounds that echo the toasted oak in Stellenbosch Cabernet and the smoky edge often present in Pinotage. Fat is the other half of the equation: lamb, boerewors, and other fattier cuts coat the palate, and tannin exists precisely to cut through that richness by binding to proteins and fat, cleansing your mouth for the next bite. Smoked meats work the same way but push further, matching Pinotage's own smoky, sometimes reductive character note for note. Avoid delicate preparations here, poached fish or light salads get flattened by the tannin and alcohol. This is food-and-wine matching by brute force rather than subtlety, built for direct heat, charred fat, and wine with enough structure to stand up to both.
