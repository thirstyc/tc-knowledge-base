What pairs with Cremant?

Crémant's bubbles and bright acidity make it one of the most food-flexible wines going, which is why it works from the aperitif hour straight through dinner. Start it with charcuterie, potato chips, or hard cheese before a meal, the carbonation cleanses the palate and the acid cuts through fat and salt. It's genuinely excellent with seafood, oysters, fried fish, ceviche, ceviche needs acid and Crémant delivers with none of Champagne's price anxiety. Rosé Crémant, often made from pinot noir, has enough body to sit through a full fine-dining tasting menu, standing in for Champagne course after course without embarrassment. It also handles fried food better than most still white wines because the bubbles physically scrub oil off your tongue between bites. Serve it properly cold, 6 to 8°C, colder than you'd pour a still white, since chill tightens the bead and keeps things crisp. Treat it as a working wine rather than a special-occasion prop and you'll reach for it far more often.
