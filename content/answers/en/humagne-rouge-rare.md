Why is Humagne Rouge hard to find outside Valais?

Valais is a narrow, near-vertical wine region wedged into the Swiss Alps, and there simply isn't much flat (or even plantable) land to go around. Humagne Rouge occupies a sliver of that sliver, an old local variety with no genetic relation to the white Humagne Blanche despite sharing a name and confusing generations of drinkers. Total plantings are tiny, yields are modest because of the steep terraced sites, and Switzerland as a whole exports very little wine to begin with. The Swiss drink most of what they make, and the best bottles rarely make it past their own borders, let alone across the Alps. Add in the labor cost of farming vertical vineyards by hand and small production runs, and you get a wine that is essentially a domestic secret. If you find a bottle outside Switzerland, it likely came home in someone's suitcase or through a very determined importer. Treat it as a souvenir wine, not something you can reliably restock.
