Why does Paarl Chenin Blanc taste so rich yet fresh?

Warmer and lower-altitude than Stellenbosch, with old bush vines that concentrate flavor naturally. Ripe stone fruit balanced by Chenin's natural acid. One of South Africa's original wine regions, still quietly excellent.
