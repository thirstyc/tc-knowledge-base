What pairs with Israeli Cabernet?

Israeli Cabernet tends to run big: ripe fruit, firm tannin, alcohol often north of 14%. That combination needs food with enough fat and protein to soften the tannin and enough char or spice to stand up to the fruit. Grilled lamb is the obvious match, especially with cumin or za'atar, since the smoke and fat cut through the tannic grip while the spice echoes the wine's warmth. Braised short rib or a good burger works the same way. Mediterranean staples like eggplant with tahini, or a tomato-based meat stew, also hold up because the acidity in the tomato balances the wine's richness. Avoid delicate fish or anything too lean, since the tannin will just feel harsh without fat to grab onto. Serve it around 16-18°C, on the cooler end of red-wine range, because these wines already run warm on the palate and a slightly lower temperature keeps the alcohol from tasting hot and lets the tannin structure show instead of just the heat.
