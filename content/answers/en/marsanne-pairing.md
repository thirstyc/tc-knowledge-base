What pairs with Marsanne?

Marsanne's waxy texture and low acidity mean it doesn't cut through fat the way a Sauvignon Blanc would, so pair it with dishes where you want the wine to match richness rather than slice through it. Roast chicken with the skin on, a cream-based mushroom risotto, or pasta in a buttery sauce all work because the wine's texture mirrors the dish instead of fighting it. Think of it less as a palate-cleanser and more as an extension of the plate. Because it lacks sharp acid, avoid anything very acidic or vinegary on the plate, like a sharp vinaigrette, which will make the wine taste dull and flabby by comparison. Roasted root vegetables, a bit of saffron, or a light curry with cream also suit it, since the spice needs a soft landing rather than a zippy counterpoint. Serve it around 12-13°C so the texture stays lively rather than flat and the aromatics don't disappear under too much warmth.
