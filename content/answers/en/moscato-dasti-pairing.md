What pairs with Moscato d'Asti?

Moscato d'Asti's low alcohol, usually around 5-6%, and gentle sweetness make it one of the few wines that actually cools down spicy food instead of fighting it, so it's a natural with Thai, Sichuan, or Indian dishes that would clash with a bigger, more tannic red. It's also a genuine dessert wine on its own terms: serve it with fruit tarts, biscotti, or anything not overly sweet itself, since a dessert richer than the wine will make it taste flat. Light, creamy appetizers, mild cheeses, or a fruit salad work well too, riding the wine's fizz and acidity rather than fighting them. Avoid pairing it with anything heavy, fatty, or aggressively savory, that's not its job. Serve it cold, around 6-8°C, and drink it young; it's built for immediate pleasure, not contemplation, and it loses its charm fast once opened, so don't expect the bottle to last a second day in the fridge.
