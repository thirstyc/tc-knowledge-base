Best wine for a dinner party?

Plan one wine for each stage of the meal, and choose crowd-pleasers that work with lots of food: sparkling to start, a crisp white, a versatile red, and something sweet for dessert.

Start with bubbles. Champagne, Crémant or Cava welcome guests and pair with almost any starter.

For the white, pick something fresh and easy to like, such as Sauvignon Blanc, Chablis or an unoaked Chardonnay.

For the red, go for a medium-bodied, food-friendly wine that won't overwhelm the dish. Pinot Noir, Côtes du Rhône and Rioja Crianza are all good choices.

For dessert, a small pour of Moscato d'Asti, Sauternes or Tawny Port finishes the evening nicely.

How much to buy: a standard 750 ml bottle gives about five glasses. Plan on roughly half a bottle per person over a long dinner, and buy an extra bottle or two. Magnums look generous and are easy to share.

A few practical tips:

- Chill whites and sparkling wine in advance.
- Open reds 30 minutes before serving.
- Serve wines from lightest to fullest.
- Keep water on the table.

If guests bring wine, you don't have to open it that night. It's a gift, not a contribution to the menu.
