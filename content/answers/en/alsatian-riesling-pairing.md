What pairs with Alsatian Riesling?

High acidity cuts fat and resets the palate, while the wine's aromatic lift and any residual sugar tame capsaicin heat - which is why it's a natural with Thai curries, Sichuan dishes, pho, or chili-forward Indian food. The fruit-forward nose (lychee, peach, white blossom) doesn't clash with spice the way tannic reds do, and the absence of oak means no vanilla fighting the chili. It's also the classic match for Alsace's own Munster and other washed-rind cheeses: acid slices through the funk and fat, and a touch of sweetness softens the salt. Choucroute, the region's sauerkraut-and-sausage dish, works for the same reason - the wine's tang mirrors the dish's. Skip delicate, subtle plates like plain steamed fish; Riesling's aromatics will steamroll them. Serve at 10-12°C so the fruit and floral notes actually show rather than freezing shut. If the bottle is genuinely dry, it plays crisper and more mineral; sweeter styles (Vendanges Tardives and beyond) hold up better against real heat.
