Why does Campania wine taste so ancient and volcanic?

Vines growing on the slopes of Vesuvius and around Avellino, some on ungrafted, phylloxera-free roots centuries old. Aglianico reds run tannic and structured; Fiano and Greco whites run nutty and mineral. Some of Italy's oldest viticulture, still thriving.
