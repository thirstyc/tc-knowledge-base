What pairs with Lagrein?

Lagrein's thick-skinned tannin and dark fruit character need food with some fat and weight to match it, or the tannin will just read as harsh. Mushroom risotto is a natural fit - the earthy umami plays nicely against the wine's dark berry and slightly bitter edge. Roasted chicken or pork works too, especially with a pan sauce, since the fat helps smooth the tannins. Alpine cheeses - the firm, nutty, mountain-hut style - are a regional pairing that makes sense for a reason: they share the same terroir logic, and their saltiness balances the wine's tannic grip. Cured meats like speck, which is practically native to the same valley, are another obvious match. What to avoid: delicate or acidic dishes, since Lagrein's tannin and body will overwhelm anything too light. Serve at 16-18°C, and don't be afraid to open it a bit before drinking - a short decant softens the tannin and lets the dark fruit open up.
