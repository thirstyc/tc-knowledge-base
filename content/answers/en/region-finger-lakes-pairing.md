What pairs with Finger Lakes Riesling?

Finger Lakes Riesling gets its backbone from geography as much as grape: the deep glacial lakes moderate the region's harsh continental climate, storing summer heat and releasing it slowly, which prevents killing frosts and lets Riesling hang long enough to ripen without losing its naturally steep acidity. That acid is the whole point on the table, it cuts straight through fat, sugar and heat instead of getting flattened by them. Spicy Asian food works because a touch of residual sugar, common in many Finger Lakes styles, tames chili heat while the acid resets your palate between bites. Roast pork gets a similar treatment, fat sliced clean by acidity. Fresh trout, a genuinely local pairing given the lakes themselves, has a delicate texture that a bone-dry version won't overwhelm. Serve it cold, around 8 to 10°C, and check whether the bottle is dry or off-dry before you pair, since the region makes both and they behave differently at the table.
