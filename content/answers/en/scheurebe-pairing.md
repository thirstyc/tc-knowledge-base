What pairs with Scheurebe?

Scheurebe's whole personality is grapefruit pith and blackcurrant leaf, with a sharp streak of acidity that can read almost aggressive if you pair it wrong. The trick is matching intensity rather than trying to soften it. Fresh goat cheese works because the tang mirrors the wine instead of fighting it. Same logic with salads dressed in vinaigrette, or asparagus, which is practically a national pairing in Germany during Spargel season since the vegetable's slight bitterness plays nicely against the citrus. Light seafood, especially anything with a squeeze of lemon already on it, holds up too. What you want to avoid is anything rich, buttery or oily, since Scheurebe has almost no fat in its own texture to bridge that gap, and the acid will just slice through and make the dish taste dull. Serve it cold, around 8 to 10 degrees, so the aromatics stay lifted rather than turning blowsy. Think of it as a wine for bright, slightly assertive food, not a wine for coasting alongside something rich.
