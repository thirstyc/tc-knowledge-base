What pairs with Refosco?

Refosco — usually Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso from Friuli, or its Slovenian and Istrian cousins — carries real tannin and bracing acidity alongside dark plum and black-pepper notes. That combination exists to cut through fat and match protein, so the pairing logic is straightforward: grilled sausage, braised beef, lamb stew, anything with rendered fat or a char on it. The tannin binds to fat and protein on the palate, softening both the wine's grip and the dish's richness, while the acidity resets your mouth for the next bite instead of leaving you feeling coated. Cured meats and hard aged cheeses work too, for the same reason. What doesn't work is anything delicate — poached fish, light salads, cream sauces — where the tannin will just steamroll the food. Serve it around 16 to 18°C, a touch below room temperature, so the tannin doesn't feel harsh. This is not a sipping wine on its own; it wants a plate in front of it, ideally one that's been on a grill.
