Wine with fruit desserts?

A sweet wine with plenty of acidity, like Moscato d'Asti, late-harvest Riesling or Sauternes. Fruit desserts are sweet and often tart, so the wine needs both sweetness and freshness to keep up.

Match the wine to the fruit.

Peach, apricot and other stone-fruit desserts pair well with Moscato, Sauternes or a late-harvest Riesling, which share the same stone-fruit flavours.

Apple and pear desserts, like tarte Tatin or apple crumble, suit a sweet Chenin Blanc from the Loire, such as Coteaux du Layon, or a Tokaji Aszú.

Berry desserts, like raspberry tart or strawberry shortcake, work beautifully with Brachetto d'Acqui, a sweet, lightly sparkling red from Piedmont.

Citrus desserts, like lemon tart, want a wine with bright acidity: Moscato d'Asti or a late-harvest Riesling.

Tropical fruit, like mango or pineapple, pairs well with late-harvest wines or ice wine, including the ice wines made in Canada's Niagara region.

Baked fruit with warm spices, like a plum crumble, suits a Tawny Port.

Fruit desserts with cream or custard can take a richer wine like Sauternes.

What to avoid: dry wines and heavy reds. They'll taste sour and bitter against the fruit.
