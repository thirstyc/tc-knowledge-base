What pairs with Willamette Pinot?

Willamette Valley Pinot Noir runs cool-climate: bright acidity, moderate tannin, and an earthy, savoury undertone that comes from the region's marine-influenced climate and volcanic and sedimentary soils. That earthiness is the key to pairing it well, since the wine is looking for dishes with the same register rather than pure richness. Duck is the classic match, the acid cutting through the fat while the wine's own gamey, forest-floor notes echo the meat. Mushroom risotto works for the same reason, umami and earth meeting earth, with the wine's acidity keeping the creaminess from feeling heavy. Salmon off the grill is the local, obvious choice, since Oregon does both well and the Pinot's acid and light tannin don't fight the fish's richness the way a bigger red would. Serve it a little cooler than a heavy red, around 14 to 16°C, so the fruit and earth stay balanced rather than the alcohol taking over. Skip anything too delicate or sweet, this wine wants a little depth to play against.
