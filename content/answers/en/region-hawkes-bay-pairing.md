What pairs with Hawke's Bay Merlot?

This is a wine built for the roasting pan, not the picnic table. Merlot from Hawke's Bay carries ripe plum and dusty tannin with enough backbone to stand up to fat and char, so roast lamb with garlic and rosemary is the obvious match, and grilled beef works just as well if you keep the sauce simple. Aged cheddar is the sleeper pick: the wine's tannin needs protein and salt to soften into something silky, and a hard, crystalline cheddar delivers both without fighting the fruit. Avoid delicate fish or anything too acidic like a tomato-heavy dish, since the wine's structure will just flatten it. Serve around 16 to 18 degrees, cooler than room temperature in most houses, so the tannin stays taut instead of turning soupy. Decant it for half an hour if it is young. Think Sunday dinner, not a light Tuesday supper.
