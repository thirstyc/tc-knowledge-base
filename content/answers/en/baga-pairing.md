What pairs with Baga?

The textbook match is leitão, Bairrada's roast suckling pig with the skin cracked and blistered, and it's not a stylistic choice so much as a functional one. Baga's tannin and searing acidity exist to cut through pork fat and crisp skin; the wine is barely comprehensible without grease on the plate. That's the regional logic, grape and dish evolved side by side. Outside that pairing, look for anything with real fat and char, grilled lamb, a slow braise, a hard aged cheese with its own bite to match the wine's. Skip delicate food, Baga flattens it without effort. Also skip drinking young Baga on its own, without food the tannin just reads as harsh and unfinished. Give it a few years and the tannin resolves enough to work with simpler roasted meats too. Serve at 16 to 18°C, standard red range, and no warmer, extra heat just makes both the alcohol and the tannin more aggressive on the palate.
