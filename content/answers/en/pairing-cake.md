Wine with cake?

A sweet wine that's at least as sweet as the cake. A dry wine next to a sweet dessert tastes sour and thin, so skip the Brut Champagne and reach for something with some sugar.

Light, fluffy cakes, like sponge, vanilla or birthday cake, pair well with Moscato d'Asti or a demi-sec Champagne. Their bubbles and sweetness keep things light.

Lemon cake suits Moscato d'Asti or a late-harvest Riesling, both with bright acidity to match the citrus.

Cheesecake is rich and tangy. Sauternes, late-harvest Riesling or Tokaji Aszú balance the creaminess.

Carrot cake, with its spices and cream cheese frosting, works well with a late-harvest Riesling, a sweet Chenin Blanc or Tokaji.

Fruitcake and Christmas cake are dense and full of dried fruit. Tawny Port, sweet Madeira or Pedro Ximénez Sherry match those flavours perfectly.

Chocolate cake calls for Banyuls, Ruby Port or a Late Bottled Vintage Port.

Coffee and walnut cake suits Tawny Port or a sweet Oloroso Sherry.

What to avoid: dry wines of any kind. Even a great bottle will taste harsh against a sweet dessert.
