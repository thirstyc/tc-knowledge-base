What pairs with Godello?

Godello's high acidity and light-to-medium body make it a natural with anything from the sea that isn't drowned in heavy sauce — grilled octopus, clams in white wine, oysters, or Galicia's own pulpo a la gallega. The acid cuts through fat and brininess the way lemon would, refreshing your palate between bites instead of getting steamrolled by them. It also works well with fresh pasta in butter or olive oil, and soft, young cheeses like a mild goat cheese, since it doesn't have the tannin or oak to clash with delicate textures. Avoid pairing it with anything too spicy or aggressively seasoned — its subtlety gets lost. Serve around 8-10°C so the acidity stays bright and doesn't taste sour or thin, which can happen if it's poured too warm. Think of it as a food wine first, sipping wine second: it's built to work alongside a plate, not to dominate a glass on its own.
