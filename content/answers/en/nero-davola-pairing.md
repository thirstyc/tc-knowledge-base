What pairs with Nero d'Avola?

The pairing logic here comes from the wine's actual balance: dark ripe fruit, real acidity, and tannin that's present but soft rather than aggressive. Grilled fish sounds odd for a red this dark, but Sicilian swordfish or tuna, cooked over charcoal with olive oil and herbs, has enough char and fat to meet the wine halfway, and the acidity keeps it from feeling heavy. Lamb, especially with rosemary or mint, works because the herbal, savory notes in the wine mirror what's on the plate while the fruit stands up to the gaminess. Mushrooms bring earthy umami that plays well against the wine's soft tannin and dark fruit without needing heavy tannic grip to hold up. Serve around 16-17°C. It's a flexible, food-friendly red precisely because it isn't aggressive, don't reach for something delicate like plain white fish or salad, but almost anything grilled, roasted, or earthy will find common ground with it.
