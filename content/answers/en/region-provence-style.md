Why does Provence rosé taste so pale and bone-dry?

Mediterranean sun balanced by cooling winds off the coast, short skin contact for barely-there color. Grenache, Cinsault, and Syrah blended for delicate red fruit, not sweetness. The template every other pale rosé is copying.
