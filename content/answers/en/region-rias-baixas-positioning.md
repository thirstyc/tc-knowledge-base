Why does Rías Baixas matter for Spanish wine?

It proved Spain could make serious, food-driven white wine, not just big reds. Galicia's cool, wet climate is the opposite of what most people picture for Spain. The go-to white for anyone who loves Muscadet or Vinho Verde.
