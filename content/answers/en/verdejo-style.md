Why does Verdejo taste so herbal with a bitter-almond edge?

Native to Rueda in Spain, picked for freshness, often with a touch of skin contact. Grassy, green-apple fruit with a distinctive bitter almond finish. Spain's answer to Sauvignon Blanc, with its own signature.
