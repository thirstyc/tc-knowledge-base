Best wine for a gift?

Pick a wine with a story rather than a famous label, and match it to the person. Champagne or another traditional-method sparkling wine is the safest choice if you don't know their taste.

A few approaches that work:

- Sparkling wine suits almost any occasion, and a Crémant or a good Cava looks generous without costing a lot.
- A magnum feels like an event, and it's more memorable than two ordinary bottles.
- A classic from a famous region is a safe bet if you know they like that style.
- A half bottle of sweet wine, like Sauternes or an ice wine, is an easy luxury.
- Something from a place they love, a region they've travelled to or want to visit, always lands well.

For a wine lover, look for something unusual rather than expensive: a grape or region they may not have tried.

For a beginner, choose something approachable, and add a short note about what to drink it with.

Practical tips: ask a good wine shop for help, keep the receipt, and consider pairing the bottle with glasses or a book.

If you're really unsure, a gift card to a good independent wine shop beats a guess.
