What pairs with Feteasca Alba?

The whole point of this wine is its acidity, so pair it with things that need a palate cleanser. Seafood works because the acid cuts through any richness or brininess without fighting it. Fresh, soft cheeses, think something like a young goat cheese, get lifted rather than weighed down, since the wine's brightness matches the cheese's own tang instead of clashing with it. Light pasta dishes, especially anything with a lemon or herb-based sauce rather than heavy cream, let the wine's white fruit and floral notes come through instead of getting buried. What you want to avoid is anything too rich or fatty on its own, a heavy cream sauce or fried food will just make the wine taste thin by comparison. This is a wine that wants food it can refresh, not food it has to compete with. Keep it cold, keep the plate light, and it does its job well.
