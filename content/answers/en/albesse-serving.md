What temperature should I serve Albesse at?

Aim for 8 to 10°C, straight out of the fridge is basically fine. Sauvignon Blanc's whole personality depends on brightness and tension, that citrusy, herbaceous snap, and if you let it warm up past that range it starts tasting flabby and one-dimensional, like the acidity fell asleep. Too cold and you mute the aromatics entirely, so don't go overboard with an ice bucket dunk right before pouring either. If the bottle's been in the fridge for a few hours, that's your sweet spot. One practical tip: pull it out about ten minutes before serving if your fridge runs colder than 4°C, since Sauvignon Blanc shows better when it's not numbingly cold. You want to actually taste the grapefruit and gooseberry notes, not just feel a cold liquid hit your tongue. This isn't a wine that needs babying or decanting, it's built to be poured and enjoyed immediately, so keep the serving simple and focus on getting that temperature right.
