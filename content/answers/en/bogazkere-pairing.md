What pairs with Bogazkere?

Boğazkere's name literally translates as "throat scratcher," and that's the mechanism you're pairing around: thick skins give it enormous tannin plus high acid, so it needs fat and protein to soften the astringency and something savoury or charred to match its intensity. Grilled lamb is the classic move, especially kebabs with char and cumin, because the fat coats the palate while the smoke echoes the wine's dark, roasted character. Turkish dishes built on grilled meat, eggplant, and spice work for the same reason. Avoid anything delicate or acidic on its own, like fish or light salads, since the tannin will flatten them. Serve around 16-18°C, a touch cooler than a typical Bordeaux-style red, so the tannin doesn't feel even more aggressive. If the tannin still feels punishing, look for bottles blended with the softer Öküzgözü grape, which is the traditional local fix and makes the wine considerably more approachable at the table.
