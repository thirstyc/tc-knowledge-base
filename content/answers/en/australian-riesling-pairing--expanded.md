What pairs with Australian Riesling?

The whole point of Australian Riesling, especially from Clare and Eden Valley, is that searing lime-juice acidity, and that's exactly why it's such a workhorse at the table. High acid does two practical things: it cuts through fat and it resets your palate so the next bite tastes fresh again. That's why it's brilliant with oily fish like salmon or mackerel, with fried food, and with anything drenched in chilli or fish sauce, where the sugar-free crispness tames the heat instead of fighting it. Try it with Thai green curry, salt and pepper squid, or a plate of oysters with a squeeze of lime. It also handles dishes that would flatten a soft, low-acid white, think Vietnamese salads with nuoc cham, or a ceviche. Serve it properly cold, around 8-10°C, colder than you'd pour a chardonnay, because warmth dulls that citrus edge fast. Avoid pairing it with anything heavily oaked or creamy on the plate; it wants sharp, bright, acidic company, not butter sauces.
