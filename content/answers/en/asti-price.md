Why is Asti so affordable?

The price comes straight from the production method. Asti is made using the Charmat, or tank, method, where the second fermentation that creates the bubbles happens in large sealed steel tanks over a couple of weeks, rather than in individual bottles over months or years like traditional method sparkling wine. That means no manual riddling, no disgorgement, no long lees aging, and far less labor per bottle. Producers can ferment huge volumes at once, control temperature precisely, and stop fermentation early to lock in sweetness and lower alcohol, typically 5-7%. Lower alcohol and shorter production time also mean lower costs and, depending on the market, sometimes lower taxes. None of this makes Asti a lesser wine, it's just built for a completely different purpose than Champagne: fast, fresh, aromatic, and meant to be drunk within a year or two of release rather than cellared. The affordability is the method working exactly as intended, not a shortcut being taken.
