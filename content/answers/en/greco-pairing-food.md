What pairs with Greco?

Greco's high acidity and saline, volcanic minerality make it a workhorse with anything oily or briny, since the acid cuts fat and the minerality echoes shellfish and cured fish rather than fighting them. Think grilled octopus, clams in white wine sauce, or a simple plate of fresh anchovies, all classic Campanian pairings that grew up alongside this grape. It also handles fresh pasta with seafood or lemon-based sauces well, because the wine's texture is fuller than you'd expect from something this acidic, giving it enough weight to stand up to cream or olive oil without getting lost. Soft, young cheeses like burrata or a fresh ricotta work too, since Greco doesn't have the tannin to clash and its acidity refreshes the palate between rich bites. Avoid pairing it with anything too delicate or floral, it'll overpower a subtle dish, and skip heavy red-sauce fare where the wine's minerality just gets buried. Serve around 10 to 12 degrees so the acid stays lively and the food pairing actually works as intended.
