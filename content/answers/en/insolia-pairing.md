What food actually works with Insolia, and what should I avoid pairing it with?

Think seafood, and specifically the lighter, simpler end of seafood. Grilled sardines, a plate of clams, raw oysters, a simple pasta with anchovies and breadcrumbs, that's Insolia's home turf. The grape's moderate acidity and almond-like bitterness on the finish echo the briny, slightly bitter notes you get in a lot of Sicilian coastal cooking, so it's less about contrast and more about matching intensity. Where it falls apart is with anything rich, creamy or heavily spiced. A butter-laden risotto or a cream sauce will flatten the wine's acidity and make it taste watery. Same problem with very spicy food, which will just steamroll the delicate fruit. This isn't a wine that fights for attention, so give it food that lets it do quiet, supporting work rather than asking it to stand up to something loud. Serve it cold, around 8 to 10°C, and drink it young. Insolia isn't built to age gracefully.
