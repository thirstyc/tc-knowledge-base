What's the Difference Between Rioja and Ribera del Duero Tempranillo?

Rioja is traditional, oak-aged (American oak), elegant, silky. Ribera del Duero is modern, cooler climate, powerful, structured. Rioja tastes like tradition. Ribera tastes like the future. Both are excellent. Rioja is older. Ribera is bolder.
