Why does Croatia matter for wine history?

DNA testing revealed Zinfandel's original ancestor grows right here on the Dalmatian coast. Thousands of years of winemaking tradition, only recently rediscovered by the wider world. A coastline of undervalued, distinctive wine hiding in plain sight.
