What pairs with Primitivo?

High alcohol wines taste warmer and slightly sweeter on the palate, which means Primitivo needs food with real fat, char or spice to hold its own against it rather than get bulldozed. Grilled and barbecued meats are the obvious match because the smoke and fat mirror the wine's richness, and the moderate acidity in Primitivo won't cut through greasiness the way a higher-acid red would, so you're relying on the food's own fat to balance things rather than the wine's structure. Spicy dishes work too, since alcohol can amplify heat from chili, so it's less about dousing spice and more about matching intensity to intensity. Tomato-based sauces and sausage are reliable weeknight pairings. What doesn't work is anything delicate or acidic, like a light salad or fish, where the wine will just taste hot and clumsy. Serve around 16°C rather than room temperature, since warmth exaggerates the alcohol burn. Think Sunday barbecue wine, not something to pair with subtlety.
