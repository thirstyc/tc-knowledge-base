Is Assyrtiko Only From Santorini?

Mostly. Santorini is home. You'll find small plantings in Paros and other Aegean islands. But 90%+ is Santorini. The volcanic terroir is the story. Other islands don't have the same intensity. So if it says Santorini PDO, you're getting the real thing. Other Assyrtiko exists but tastes tamer. Terroir matters here more than anywhere.
