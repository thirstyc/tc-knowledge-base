Why Does Sangiovese Require Food?

High tannin, high acidity. On its own, it's aggressive. Harsh. Grippy. Pair it with food—especially fatty, salty, or savory—and it transforms. The food tames the tannin. The wine cuts the richness. It's the ultimate food wine. Never drink Sangiovese alone.
