Wine for Super Bowl Sunday?

Crowd-pleasing, food-friendly wines that handle wings, chips and chili: juicy reds, off-dry whites and plenty of bubbles.

Game-day food is salty, spicy, fried and rich, so the wine needs fruit and freshness rather than finesse.

What works:

- Buffalo wings: off-dry Riesling or a fruity rosé. The sweetness balances the heat.
- Chili: Zinfandel, Malbec or a Grenache blend.
- Burgers and sliders: Zinfandel, Malbec, Shiraz or Tempranillo.
- Nachos and guacamole: Sauvignon Blanc, Albariño or a dry rosé.
- Fried snacks: sparkling wine. Bubbles cut through grease better than almost anything.
- Pizza: Barbera, Chianti or Lambrusco.
- Barbecue ribs and pulled pork: Zinfandel or Shiraz.

Practical tips:

- Go for magnums or several bottles of a couple of styles rather than lots of different ones.
- Screwcaps save time.
- Keep a few lower-alcohol options for a long afternoon.
- Put reds in the fridge for 15–20 minutes. Big reds taste better slightly cool, especially with spicy food.

And keep water and non-alcoholic options on hand, especially for anyone driving home.
