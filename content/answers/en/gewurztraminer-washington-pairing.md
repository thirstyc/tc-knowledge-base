What food actually works with Washington Gewürztraminer, beyond the usual 'pairs with everything spicy' line?

The pairing logic isn't really about heat, it's about the wine's aromatic weight matching food that's also aromatic and slightly sweet or fatty. Think Thai basil chicken, Vietnamese salad rolls with peanut sauce, or a pork banh mi, the wine's lychee and ginger notes echo what's already in the dish instead of fighting it. Any lingering sugar in the wine also does real work against chili heat, sugar physically dulls capsaicin's burn on your palate better than tannin or alcohol ever will, so a slightly off-dry bottle is genuinely functional with a green curry, not just a cute suggestion. It also holds up well with soft washed-rind cheeses or pâté, where the wine's floral lift cuts through fat instead of getting flattened by it. What doesn't work is anything tomato-heavy or a big grilled steak, the wine has no tannin structure to stand up to red meat and the acidity is too gentle to cut through tomato's own acid. Keep the food aromatic and a little rich, and this grape earns its reputation honestly.
