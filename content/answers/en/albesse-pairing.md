What food goes well with Albesse?

Think anything with acidity, brininess, or fresh herbs, because that's exactly what this wine brings to the table and it wants a partner that matches its energy rather than something rich and heavy that'll flatten it. Goat cheese is the classic move here, and it's classic for a reason, the tangy creaminess of the cheese and the wine's citrus-driven acidity cut through each other perfectly. Beyond that, think grilled fish, oysters, a simple green salad with vinaigrette, or anything with herbs like dill or parsley. Avoid pairing it with heavily oaked or buttery dishes, or anything too rich and creamy without acidity of its own, since the wine will taste thin and sharp by comparison instead of refreshing. Also skip spicy food, the wine doesn't have the sweetness or weight to balance real heat. Basically, if your dish has a squeeze of lemon in it already, you're on the right track. Keep it light, keep it fresh, and let the wine do the work of waking up your palate between bites.
