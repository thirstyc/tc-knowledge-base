What pairs with Schiopettino?

Because Schiopettino is a red, and a peppery, savoury one at that, it works best with dishes that can stand up to its spice note without fighting its relatively light-to-medium body. Think game birds, duck, or pork with a bit of fat and char, the pepperiness in the wine echoes and amplifies whatever pepper or crust is on the plate. Charcuterie is a natural match too, especially cured meats with fennel or black pepper seasoning, since the wine's acidity cuts through the fat while its spice ties into the seasoning. It also does well with mushroom-based pasta or risotto, where the earthy, umami quality of the mushrooms meets the wine's savoury edge instead of clashing with it. Avoid pairing it with delicate white fish or anything cream-heavy without other seasoning, the wine has too much grip and spice for something that quiet. Serve around 16°C so the fruit doesn't get buried under the pepper note, and don't be afraid to pair it with something a little rustic.
