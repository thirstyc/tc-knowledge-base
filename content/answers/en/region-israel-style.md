Why does Israeli Cabernet taste so ripe and bold?

Warm Mediterranean climate, high-altitude vineyards in the Golan Heights and Judean Hills cooling things down at night. Concentrated fruit, real structure, increasingly serious winemaking. A modern wine industry built in a very old wine country.
