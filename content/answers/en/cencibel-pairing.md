What pairs with Cencibel?

This is a wine built for smoke and char, so treat it that way. The high alcohol and ripe, jammy fruit need something with enough fat and intensity to match them, otherwise the wine just tastes hot and one-dimensional. Grilled lamb, chorizo, morcilla, anything off a live-fire grill — the charred, smoky flavors echo the wine's own baked-fruit character instead of clashing with it. Manchego cheese is another natural partner, its salt and nuttiness balancing the wine's richness. Where Cencibel falls flat is with anything delicate or acidic — a light salad or a squeeze of lemon on fish will just expose the wine's low acidity and make it taste flabby. Serve it around 16 to 18°C rather than fully at room temperature; too warm and the alcohol starts to dominate over the fruit. Think rustic, hearty, smoky food, and the wine does exactly what it's supposed to.
