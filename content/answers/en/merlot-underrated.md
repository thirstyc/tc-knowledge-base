Why did Merlot's reputation take a hit?

A movie mocked it, and casual drinkers ran the other way. Right Bank Bordeaux — Pomerol, Saint-Émilion — is built almost entirely on Merlot and commands serious prices. The grape never stopped being excellent, the trend just moved on.
