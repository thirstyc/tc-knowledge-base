What pairs with Furmint?

Furmint's whole personality is acid and texture, so it wants food that's either fatty, rich, or spiced enough to need cutting. Hungarian paprikash and goulash are the obvious local match — the acidity slices through the fat in the sauce the same way a squeeze of lemon would, while the wine's weight (Furmint isn't thin, just precise) stands up to the paprika's heat instead of getting flattened by it. It's also excellent with fried or fatty fish — think trout, mackerel, or anything battered — because that same acid resets your palate between bites. Sheep's and goat's milk cheeses work well too, since the wine's brightness balances their tang rather than competing with it. Avoid pairing it with delicate steamed vegetables or anything too subtle; the acidity will just steamroll them. Serve at 10-12°C, cold enough to keep the citrus and mineral notes sharp but not so cold you mute the wine's texture, which is part of what makes it worth drinking in the first place.
