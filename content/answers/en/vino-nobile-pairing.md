What pairs with Vino Nobile?

The tannin is the reason for the pairing rule, not just a suggestion. Tannin binds to protein and fat, which softens its grip on your palate and makes the wine taste smoother and rounder than it does on its own. That's why Vino Nobile wants fatty, well-marbled, often gamey food: bistecca alla fiorentina, wild boar ragù, roasted lamb, anything with real char from the grill. The char matters too, since the same smoky, slightly bitter compounds in seared meat echo the wine's own structure instead of clashing with it. Aged pecorino works for the same reason, its salt and fat cutting through the tannin, and porcini mushrooms bring an earthy note that matches the wine's savory, dried-herb side. Skip delicate fish, cream sauces, or anything too light; the tannin will flatten them and taste harsh doing it. This is a wine that needs something substantial to push against, not something to whisper alongside.
