What's the Difference Between Barolo and Barbaresco?

Same grape (Nebbiolo). Barolo is more powerful, higher tannin, needs more age (10–15 years). Barbaresco is slightly more elegant, less tannin, drinks earlier (5–10 years). Barolo = King. Barbaresco = Queen. Both are royalty. Your patience level?
