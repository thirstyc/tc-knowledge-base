Wine with ice cream?

Honestly, it's tricky. Cold numbs your palate, and ice cream is very sweet, so most wines taste thin or sour next to it. The ones that work are sweet, rich and full of flavour.

Pedro Ximénez Sherry is the classic answer. It's intensely sweet, with raisin, fig and molasses flavours, and it's traditionally poured straight over vanilla ice cream in Spain.

Other good matches:

- Vanilla ice cream: Pedro Ximénez, Tawny Port or sweet Madeira.
- Chocolate ice cream: Ruby Port, Banyuls or a Late Bottled Vintage Port.
- Coffee or caramel ice cream: Tawny Port or a sweet Oloroso Sherry.
- Fruit sorbets: Moscato d'Asti, Brachetto d'Acqui or a late-harvest Riesling.
- Berry ice cream: Brachetto d'Acqui, whose red-fruit flavours match it well.

A simple trick is to pour the wine over the ice cream rather than drinking it alongside. PX and Tawny Port are especially good this way.

What to avoid: dry wines of any kind, including dry sparkling wine. The sugar makes them taste harsh and sour.

Served very cold, sweet wines work better with frozen desserts, so chill them well.
