What pairs with Ratafia?

Treat Ratafia like a sweet fortified aperitif rather than a wine that needs food to make sense of it — plenty of people in Champagne drink it on its own before a meal. That said, its honeyed sweetness and grapey richness work well against fat and salt: foie gras is the classic pairing, since the wine's sugar and alcohol cut through the richness without fighting it. It's also good with blue cheese, dried fruit and nuts, or poured over melon as a simple starter the way some Champenois do. On the dessert end, it pairs naturally with anything fruit-based — tarts, poached pears, crème brûlée — since it's essentially concentrated grape flavor rather than a fermented, acid-driven wine. Serve it chilled, 8-10°C. Don't try to match it with anything delicate; it will flatten a subtle dish. It wants richness or sweetness on the other side of the plate to balance it.
