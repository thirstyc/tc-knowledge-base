What pairs with Sauternes?

Stop saving it for dessert, it's wasted there half the time. The classic pairing is foie gras, and it works because the wine's acidity cuts through the fat while the sweetness matches the richness rather than fighting it. Blue cheese, especially something like Roquefort, does the same trick from the savoury side: salt and sweet is one of the oldest pairing tricks in the book. Spicy food is the other underused move, Thai or Sichuan dishes with real heat, because sugar tames capsaicin and the wine's weight can stand up to strong spicing without disappearing. Where it struggles is with actual dessert, especially anything chocolate or very sweet, since the dessert can just overpower the wine and make it taste thin by comparison. Best served cold, not cellar temperature, so the sweetness stays lifted rather than cloying. Think of it as a wine for contrast, not for doubling down on sugar.
