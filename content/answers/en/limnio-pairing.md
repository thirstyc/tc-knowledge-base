What pairs with Limnio?

Limnio's whole appeal is savoury restraint, so pair it with food that has earthy or umami depth rather than pure richness. Grilled lamb is the classic match: the smoke and fat play against the wine's medium tannin, which is firm enough to cut through fat but not so aggressive it fights the meat. Wild mushrooms work for the same reason, their earthiness essentially rhymes with the wine's own mineral, forest-floor character instead of competing with it. It also does well with grilled vegetables, lentil dishes, or anything with a charred crust, since that bitterness meshes with the tannin rather than clashing. Avoid pairing it with anything very sweet or heavily creamy, the wine's structure is built for savoury contrast, not richness, and it'll taste thin next to a cream sauce. Serve around 15-16°C, on the cool side for a red, which keeps the fruit tight and lets the earthy, mineral notes stay the focus at the table.
