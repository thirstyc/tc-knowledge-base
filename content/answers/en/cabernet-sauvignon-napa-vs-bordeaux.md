What's the Difference Between Napa and Bordeaux Cabernet?

Napa is riper. Warmer climate = riper grapes = higher alcohol, riper fruit. Bordeaux blends in Cabernet Franc and Merlot, diluting the intensity. Napa is pure power. Bordeaux is complexity from blending. Napa drinks younger. Bordeaux needs more time. Both age well. Napa = bold, fruit-forward. Bordeaux = elegant, structured. Your preference.
