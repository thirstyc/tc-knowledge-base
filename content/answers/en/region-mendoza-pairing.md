What pairs with Mendoza Malbec?

Mendoza's high-altitude vineyards, many over 900 metres in the shadow of the Andes, give Malbec intense sun exposure during the day and cold nights, which builds thick skins, deep color and ripe, substantial tannin without losing acidity. That combination is exactly what makes steak the natural match: the char and fat of a grilled cut soften the tannin on contact, while the wine's acidity cuts back through the richness so neither the meat nor the wine sits heavy. Empanadas work for similar reasons on a smaller scale, the pastry's fat and the seasoned beef filling giving the tannin something to grip. Grilled vegetables or smoky charred anything play off the wine's own oak-driven notes of clove and dark chocolate, common in Malbecs aged in French or American barrels. Serve it around 16-17°C, slightly cooler than room temperature, so the fruit stays fresh and the tannin doesn't read as heavy. This isn't a delicate pairing wine, it wants fire and fat.
