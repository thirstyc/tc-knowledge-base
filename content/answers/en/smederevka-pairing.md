What pairs with Smederevka?

Smederevka's bright acidity and light body make it a natural match for food you don't want weighed down. Simple grilled or pan-fried fish is the obvious call—the wine's citrus cuts through oil the way a squeeze of lemon would. Fresh, unaged cheeses (think a mild feta-style or young sheep's cheese, common across the Balkans) mirror its tangy freshness rather than clashing with it. Light pasta dishes, especially anything with lemon, herbs or a simple olive oil base, work well for the same reason. It's not a wine built to stand up to richness or heavy spice—there's no oak, no fruit concentration, and not much tannin-adjacent structure to lean on, so anything too bold will just overwhelm it. Treat it as a warm-weather, easy-drinking white for lighter meals rather than a dinner-party centerpiece. Serve cold, 8-10°C, and drink it on the younger side, since it's not built for cellaring.
