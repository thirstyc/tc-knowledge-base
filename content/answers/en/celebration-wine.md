Wine for a celebration?

Sparkling wine, almost always. Champagne if the occasion calls for it, but Crémant, Cava, English sparkling wine and Franciacorta can be just as good for much less.

Why bubbles? They feel festive, they work as an aperitif and with most food, and they suit a crowd because almost everyone enjoys a glass.

Choose the level to match the moment:

- Big party: Prosecco or Cava, easy and affordable.
- Dinner or special toast: Crémant or a good English sparkling wine.
- Milestone: Champagne, maybe a vintage bottle or a magnum.

Magnums make any celebration feel bigger, and sparkling wine arguably ages better in them.

Beyond bubbles, think about the meal. A celebratory dinner might move from Champagne to a special red, like a good Burgundy or a Barolo with some age, and finish with a sweet wine.

Practical tips:

- Chill sparkling wine well, around 6–8°C.
- Open it slowly, with a gentle sigh rather than a loud pop, to keep the bubbles.
- Pour into a normal wine glass rather than a narrow flute to smell it properly.
- Plan on about five glasses per bottle.

The best celebration wine is the one people remember drinking together.
