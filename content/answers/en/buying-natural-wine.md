Best natural wine?

The best natural wine for you depends on the style, and the smartest way to start is with regions and styles known for clean, approachable wines.

Regions with a strong tradition:

- Beaujolais: juicy, chillable Gamay, often a great introduction.
- Loire Valley: Chenin Blanc, Cabernet Franc and light reds.
- Jura: oxidative whites and pale, perfumed reds.
- Languedoc and Roussillon: warm, generous reds and skin-contact whites at good prices.
- Catalonia, Sicily and southern Italy: characterful, often very affordable wines.
- Georgia: amber wines fermented in clay qvevri, the original skin-contact style.
- Chile's Itata Valley: old-vine País and Cinsault.

Good styles to start with:

- Chillable light reds, which are fresh and forgiving.
- Pét-nat, the fizzy, often cloudy sparkling style.
- Skin-contact or orange wine, once you're comfortable.

The best advice: buy from a shop or bar that tastes what it sells and will tell you honestly which bottles are clean and which are funky. Natural wine varies more than conventional wine, and a good retailer is your best filter.

If a bottle is faulty, say so. Good natural wine shouldn't need excuses.
