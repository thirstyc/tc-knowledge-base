What pairs with Alsace Riesling or Gewürztraminer?

Choucroute is the classic pairing for a reason: the wine's high acidity and faint sweetness cut through the fat of sausage and cured pork while its aromatics stand up to the tang of the sauerkraut. That same structure makes it excellent with spicy Asian food, a touch of residual sugar tempers chili heat far better than a dry, tannic red ever could, and the lychee and ginger notes in Gewürztraminer actually echo flavors already on the plate. Rich pâté works too, the acidity slices through the fat instead of getting buried by it. Serve both around 10 to 12°C, cold enough to keep the aromatics from turning blowsy but not so cold you mute them entirely. The mistake people make is treating these wines as delicate sippers when they're actually built to handle assertive, even confrontational food. Don't save Alsace whites for quiet solo drinking, pour them next to something with real flavor and let them do their job.
