Why does Uruguayan Tannat taste so much softer than French Tannat?

Atlantic-moderated climate takes the edge off the grape's notoriously aggressive tannin. Dark fruit, earthy structure, still serious but more approachable young. Uruguay adopted a rustic French grape and made it its own.
