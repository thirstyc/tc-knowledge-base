What pairs with Malvasia Bianca?

Malvasia Bianca's sugar and gentle acidity make it a natural match for spicy food, since residual sweetness and a cold pour both blunt capsaicin burn — think Thai or Sichuan dishes where a bone-dry white would just amplify the heat. It also works with genuine dessert, but match intensity: a rich, custardy or caramel-forward dessert needs the wine's sugar to at least equal the dish's, or the wine tastes thin and sour by comparison. Fresh fruit tarts, poached pears or a mild blue cheese with honey are safer bets than chocolate, which tends to flatten delicate florals entirely. Serve around 8-10°C, and skip anything acidic or vinegar-forward, like a sharp vinaigrette, since Malvasia's own acidity usually can't keep pace and ends up tasting flabby next to it. The general rule with any sweet wine holds here too: the wine should always be at least as sweet as what's on the plate.
