Best wine for a wedding?

Choose crowd-pleasers rather than showstoppers: a sparkling wine for the toast, one versatile white, one versatile red, and plenty of each.

For the toast, sparkling wine is traditional. Crémant, Cava, Prosecco or English sparkling wine give the same moment as Champagne for a fraction of the price, which adds up fast across a guest list.

For the meal, pick wines most people will enjoy and that work with a range of dishes. Good choices include Sauvignon Blanc, an unoaked or lightly oaked Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Côtes du Rhône and Rioja Crianza. Avoid anything extreme: very tannic, very oaky or very sweet.

How much to buy is the most common question. A rough guide:

- One glass of sparkling wine per guest for the toast, about six glasses per bottle.
- Around half a bottle per guest for a reception with dinner, adjusted for your crowd.
- A little extra, since running out is worse than leftovers.

Practical tips:

- Buy by the case for discounts, and ask about sale-or-return.
- Taste your choices with the menu before committing.
- Arrange enough chilling for whites and sparkling wine.
- Magnums look generous and are easy to share.

Your venue's corkage and supply rules may decide much of this, so check them first.
