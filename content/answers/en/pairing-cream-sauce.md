Wine with cream sauce?

A full-bodied white with good acidity, like Chardonnay, white Burgundy or Chenin Blanc. Cream sauces are rich and heavy, so the wine needs enough body to match them and enough acidity to keep each bite fresh.

Oaked Chardonnay is the classic choice. Its buttery, creamy texture mirrors the sauce, while a white Burgundy adds a little more acidity and elegance.

Chenin Blanc, especially from Vouvray or South Africa, balances richness and freshness very well. Viognier is another option for creamy dishes with a bit of spice.

The rest of the dish matters too.

Chicken or pork in cream sauce suits Chardonnay or Chenin Blanc.

Seafood in cream sauce, like scallops or salmon, pairs well with Chablis or a white Burgundy.

Mushroom cream sauce opens the door to red: Pinot Noir's earthy notes are a lovely match.

Creamy pasta, like fettuccine Alfredo, works well with a lightly oaked Chardonnay or an Italian white like Soave or Fiano.

A cream sauce with lots of herbs or lemon can take a crisper white like Sauvignon Blanc.

What to avoid: tannic reds. Tannin and cream clash, leaving the wine tasting bitter and the sauce heavy.
