Why does Turkish Boğazkere taste so rustic and tannic?

High-altitude Anatolian vineyards, ancient native grapes most of the world has never heard of. Thick-skinned, high-tannin, built for long aging. One of the oldest wine-growing regions on the planet, largely overlooked.
