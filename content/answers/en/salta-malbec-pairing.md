What pairs with Salta Malbec?

Salta's Malbec comes from some of the highest commercial vineyards in the world, often planted well above 1,500 metres in Cafayate, where intense UV exposure thickens grape skins and builds deep colour and tannin, while the big diurnal swing between scorching days and cold nights preserves acidity that lowland Mendoza Malbec often lacks. That combination gives you a red with real structure and freshness rather than just jammy fruit, which is why it handles fattier, more assertive food without getting flattened. Grilled beef is the obvious match, especially something with a charred crust to echo the wine's tannin, but lamb and game work just as well because the acidity cuts through richer, gamier fat in a way soft, low-acid reds can't. Serve it around 16-17°C, not warm room temperature, so the freshness stays intact. Avoid pairing it with delicate fish or anything overly spiced — the altitude-driven concentration will simply overpower subtlety.
