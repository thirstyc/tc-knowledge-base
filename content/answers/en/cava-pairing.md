What pairs with Cava?

Cava's whole job at the table is to cut through fat and salt, so lean into that. Fried food is the classic match for a reason: the bubbles scrub the palate clean between bites of croquetas, calamari or patatas bravas, and the acidity resets your tongue so the next bite tastes as good as the first. Jamón, olives and salty almonds work the same way, playing off the wine's brightness rather than fighting it. It's also a genuinely good aperitivo pour, since it wakes up the appetite instead of filling you up the way a heavier wine would. Where it struggles is with very sweet desserts, which will make even a good Cava taste thin and sour by comparison — save those for a sweeter sparkling style instead. Keep it cold, pour it often, and don't overthink the pairing: this is a wine built for grazing, not for a single perfect plate.
