What pairs with Stellenbosch Cabernet?

Stellenbosch Cabernet has firm tannins and a savoury, slightly earthy edge from the granite and decomposed sandstone soils, so it wants fat and char to soften it. A well-marbled grilled steak or lamb chops charred over open flame let the tannins bind to protein and fat, which is exactly how tannin is supposed to work at the table, smoothing out rather than gripping your tongue. Cape Malay dishes, with their cumin, coriander and slow-braised meat, play beautifully against the wine's dark-fruit core and pick up on its spice notes without clashing. Avoid delicate fish or anything too acidic on its own, the tannins will flatten it. Aged Gouda or a hard sheep's cheese also works if you're doing a cheese board instead of a main. Serve at 16-18°C, slightly cooler than room temperature, so the alcohol doesn't dominate. Give a younger bottle 30-45 minutes of air before pouring.
