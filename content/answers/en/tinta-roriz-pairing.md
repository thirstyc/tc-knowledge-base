What pairs with Tinta Roriz?

Tinta Roriz is Tempranillo grown in the Douro, usually blended with Touriga Nacional and Touriga Franca for dry reds rather than Port, and it brings real tannic backbone to the mix. That tannin is why food matters: protein and fat bind to tannin and soften how astringent it feels on the palate, while the wine's acidity cuts back through the richness so nothing feels heavy. Grilled meats are the obvious match - lamb, beef, chouriço off the coals - but it's just as happy next to a slow-cooked stew, feijoada, or anything built on paprika and smoked pork, which are Portuguese kitchen staples for a reason. Aged cheese works too, since fat again tames the grip. Serve it around 16-18°C; too cold and the tannin turns harsh and metallic. Avoid delicate dishes, raw fish, anything light - the wine will flatten them. Basically, treat it like you'd treat a good Rioja: feed it something substantial and let the tannin do its job.
