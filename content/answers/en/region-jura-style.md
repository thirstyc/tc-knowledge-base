Why does Jura wine taste so oxidative and nutty?

Savagnin aged under a thin layer of yeast, similar to sherry, develops walnut and curry notes on purpose. Poulsard and Trousseau reds run pale, earthy, and light-bodied. A tiny region doing nothing like anywhere else in France.
