What pairs with Moscato?

Sweetness is the mechanism here: it directly counters capsaicin heat, so Moscato is genuinely one of the better matches for Thai, Sichuan or anything with real chili kick, and the low alcohol (5-7%) means it won't fan the flames the way a bigger, boozier wine can. It also works with fresh fruit desserts, light custards or a simple fruit tart, though anything too sugary will make the wine taste thin by comparison. Avoid rich chocolate or heavily caramelized desserts, they'll flatten it. Because there's little tannin or oak to bury under food, it's just as happy poured on its own as an aperitif or a lazy afternoon sipper. Serve it properly cold, around 6-8C, since chilling keeps the sweetness from tipping into cloying. It's not a wine that's trying to be serious, and matching it with equally unpretentious food, spicy street food, fruit, a hot afternoon, is really the whole point.
