## Overview

Wine Region / France

Jura

A tiny region doing nothing like anywhere else in France. Oxidative Savagnin, pale Poulsard and Trousseau reds, and Vin Jaune aged for years under a film of yeast.

Key Grapes: Savagnin, Poulsard, Trousseau, Chardonnay
Climate: Cool continental
Known For: Vin Jaune, low-intervention winemaking
Homes: Eastern France, near Switzerland
