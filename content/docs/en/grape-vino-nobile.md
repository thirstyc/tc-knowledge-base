## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Vino Nobile

Sangiovese Montepulciano. High tannin, high acidity. Structured, age-worthy. Tuscany's second-tier classic. Serious wine, friendly price.

Also Called: Sangiovese di Montepulciano
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Montepulciano (Italy)
