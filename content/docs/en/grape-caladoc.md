## Overview

Red Grape / France

Caladoc

French hybrid. Medium tannin, fruit-forward, warm-climate grape. Full-bodied, spicy. Southern France. The crossover between Old and New World styles.

Also Called: Caladoc Noir
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Southern France, Languedoc
