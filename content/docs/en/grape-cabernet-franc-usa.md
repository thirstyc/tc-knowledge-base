## Overview

Red Grape / USA

Cabernet Franc (USA)

US Cabernet Franc. Similar to Loire but riper. Medium tannin, high acidity. Cool-climate sites show elegance, warm sites show power. Finger Lakes and Hudson Valley specialty.

Also Called: Cabernet Franc
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Finger Lakes (USA), Hudson Valley
