## Overview

Red Grape / Argentina

Bonarda

Argentine red. High tannin, high alcohol. Full-bodied, dark. San Juan. The Argentine workhorse. Affordable power.

Also Called: Bonarda Mendocina
Typical Alcohol: 14–16%
Tannin: High
Homes: San Juan (Argentina)
