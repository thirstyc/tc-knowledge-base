## Overview

Red Grape / Chile

Carménère

Chile's flagship. Medium-high tannin, ripe fruit, spice. Bordeaux left this behind; Chile found it in the 1990s and built an identity on it. The grape that gave Chile its own story.

Also Called: Gros Cabernet, Liber Papi
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Maipo Valley (Chile), Central Chile
