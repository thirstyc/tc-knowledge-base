## Overview

White Grape / France

Chenin Blanc

The shape-shifter. Dry to sweet, still to sparkling. High acidity, mineral. Loire's most versatile. Low alcohol when dry. The wine that teaches you terroir. Timeless.

Also Called: Pineau de la Loire
Typical Alcohol: 10–14%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Loire Valley (France), Vouvray
