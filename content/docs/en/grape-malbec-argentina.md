## Overview

Red Grape / Argentina

Malbec (Argentine High-Altitude)

Argentina's pride. High altitude = cool ripening = structure. Dark, structured, fruit-forward. Medium-high tannin. The New World got this right. Mendoza's treasure.

Also Called: Côt (France)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Mendoza (Argentina), altitude 3,000–5,000ft
