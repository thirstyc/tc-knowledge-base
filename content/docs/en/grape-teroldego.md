## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Teroldego

Alpine red from Trentino. High acidity, medium tannin. Fresh, mineral, peppery. Cool-climate expression. The mountain wine.

Also Called: Teroldego Rotaliano
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Trentino (Italy), Campo Rotaliano
