## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Ribolla Gialla

Friuli white. High acidity, medium alcohol. Mineral, crisp. Ancient Roman. Orange wine friendly. The Friuli classic.

Also Called: Rebula (Slovenia)
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Friuli (Italy)
