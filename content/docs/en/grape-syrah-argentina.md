## Overview

Red Grape / Argentina

Syrah (Argentina)

Argentine Syrah. High tannin, high alcohol. Spicy, structured. High altitude. The Argentine alternative to Malbec.

Also Called: Syrah
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Mendoza (Argentina)
