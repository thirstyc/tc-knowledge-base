## Overview

White Grape / New Zealand

Chardonnay (New Zealand)

NZ Chardonnay. Medium-high acidity, medium-high alcohol. Stone fruit, mineral. Cooler than California. The elegant antipodean expression.

Also Called: Chardonnay
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: High
Homes: Hawke's Bay (New Zealand)
