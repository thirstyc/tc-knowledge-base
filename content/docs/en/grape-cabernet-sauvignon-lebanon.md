## Overview

Red Grape / Lebanon

Cabernet Sauvignon (Lebanon)

Lebanese Cabernet. High tannin, medium-high alcohol. Structured, age-worthy. Bekaa Valley. The Middle Eastern classic.

Also Called: Cabernet Sauvignon
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Bekaa Valley (Lebanon)
