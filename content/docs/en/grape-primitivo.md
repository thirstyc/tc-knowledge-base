## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Primitivo

Southern Italian powerhouse. High alcohol, ripe fruit, jammy. The original Zinfandel. Full-bodied, spicy, warm-climate grape that doesn't apologize. Puglia's gift to the wine world.

Also Called: Zinfandel (USA)
Typical Alcohol: 14–16%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Puglia (Italy), Salento
