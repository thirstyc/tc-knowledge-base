## Overview

Red Grape / France

Côtes du Rhône

Southern Rhône blend. Grenache-based (60–80%), Syrah, Mourvèdre. Medium tannin, high alcohol, generous. The everyday Southern Rhône. Serious wine, friendly price.

Also Called: Côtes du Rhône Villages
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Southern Rhône (France)
