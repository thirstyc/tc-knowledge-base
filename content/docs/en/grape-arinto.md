## Overview

White Grape / Portugal

Arinto

Portuguese white. Very high acidity, medium alcohol. Citrus, mineral. Fresh, age-worthy. Bucelas. The mineral Portuguese white.

Also Called: Arinto de Bucelas
Typical Alcohol: 11–12%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Bucelas (Portugal)
