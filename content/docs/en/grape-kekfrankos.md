## Overview

Red Grape / Hungary

Kékfrankos

Hungarian Blaufränkisch. High tannin, high acidity. Spicy, structured. Egri Bikavér base. The Hungarian red. Age-worthy.

Also Called: Blaufränkisch (Austria)
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Eger (Hungary)
