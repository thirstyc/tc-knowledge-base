## Overview

White Grape / Germany

Silvaner

German white. Medium acidity, low alcohol. Dry, mineral, herbal. Underrated. Alsace. The German white that tastes like earth.

Also Called: Sylvaner, Silvaner
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Germany, Alsace (France)
