## Overview

Red Grape / USA

Cabernet Sauvignon (Napa)

Napa Cabernet. High tannin, medium-high alcohol. Ripe fruit, power. The benchmark New World Cabernet. The gold standard.

Also Called: Cabernet Sauvignon
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Napa Valley (USA)
