## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Prosecco

Sparkling white wine. Low alcohol (11%), floral, fruity. Glera grape base. The party wine. Charmat method (tank fermented), not Champagne-style. Fun, affordable, Italian.

Also Called: Glera (grape name)
Typical Alcohol: 11%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Veneto (Italy), Valdobbiadene
