## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Rosso di Montalcino

Young Brunello. Medium-high tannin, high acidity. Fresh, bright. The approachable version. Proof that Sangiovese drinks young.

Also Called: Sangiovese Grosso (young)
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Montalcino (Italy)
