## Overview

Red Grape / South Africa

Pinotage

South African blend (Pinot Noir + Cinsaut). Medium tannin, full-bodied, unique. Banana, cherry, smoke. Polarizing—love it or hate it. The wine that tastes like where it's from.

Also Called: Pinotage
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: South Africa, Stellenbosch
