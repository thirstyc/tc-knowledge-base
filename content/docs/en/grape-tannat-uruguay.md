## Overview

Red Grape / Uruguay

Tannat (Uruguay)

Uruguayan Tannat. Very high tannin, very high alcohol. Structured, age-worthy. Uruguay's pride. The heavyweight champion.

Also Called: Harriague (Uruguay)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Canelones (Uruguay)
