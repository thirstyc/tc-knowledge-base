## Overview

White Grape / Greece

Rhoditis

Greek white. High acidity, low-medium alcohol. Fresh, crisp, mineral. Retsina base. Simple, clean, refreshing. The Greek everyday white.

Also Called: Roditis
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: High
Homes: Greece, Patras
