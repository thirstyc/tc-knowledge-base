## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Falanghina

Campanian white. High acidity, medium alcohol, mineral. Citrus, stone. Ancient Roman grape. Proof that history tastes good.

Also Called: Falanghina del Sannio
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Campania (Italy)
