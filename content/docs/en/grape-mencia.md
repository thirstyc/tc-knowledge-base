## Overview

Red Grape / Spain

Mencía

Northwest Spanish red. High acidity, medium tannin, structured. Cool-climate red that ages well. Bierzo's signature. Fresh, mineral, elegant. Proof that northern Spain is serious.

Also Called: Jaen (Portugal)
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Bierzo (Spain), Castile-León
