## Overview

White Grape / France

Petit Manseng

Southwest France white. Very high acidity, low alcohol. Dry, mineral, age-worthy. Madiran. The structured Southwest white.

Also Called: Petit Manseng
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Southwest France
