## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Greco

Southern Italian white. Medium alcohol, medium acidity, mineral. Stone fruit, citrus. Greco di Tufo's claim to fame. Seriously underrated outside Italy.

Also Called: Greco Bianco
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Campania (Italy), Greco di Tufo
