## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Prosecco (Higher Quality)

Quality Prosecco from Valdobbiadene/Cartizze. More complexity, better bubbles. Still light (11%), but with depth. The sparkling that deserves respect.

Also Called: Prosecco Superiore
Typical Alcohol: 11%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Veneto (Italy), Valdobbiadene
