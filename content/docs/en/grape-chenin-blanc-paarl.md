## Overview

White Grape / South Africa

Chenin Blanc (Paarl)

Paarl Chenin Blanc. High acidity, medium alcohol. Dry, mineral, complex. South African home. Proof South Africa does serious whites.

Also Called: Steen (South African name)
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: High
Homes: Paarl (South Africa)
