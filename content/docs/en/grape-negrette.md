## Overview

Red Grape / France

Negrette

Southwest France red. Medium tannin, high acidity. Floral, peppery. Toulouse region. The elegant French red. Rare outside France.

Also Called: Negrette de Toulouse
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Southwest France, Gaillac
