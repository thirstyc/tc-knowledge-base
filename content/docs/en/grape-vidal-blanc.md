## Overview

White Grape / USA

Vidal Blanc

American hybrid. Medium acidity, medium alcohol. Fresh, crisp. Ice wine base. Simple, approachable, fun.

Also Called: Vidal, Vidal Blanc
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Finger Lakes (USA), Canada
