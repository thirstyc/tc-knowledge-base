## Overview

Red Grape / Austria

Blaufränkisch

Austrian red. High tannin, high acidity. Spicy, structured, age-worthy. Burgenland. The serious Austrian red.

Also Called: Frankovka (Czech), Kékfrankos (Hungary)
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Burgenland (Austria)
