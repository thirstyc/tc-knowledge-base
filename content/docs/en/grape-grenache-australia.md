## Overview

Red Grape / Australia

Grenache (Australia)

Australian Grenache. Old-vine expressions show depth. Medium-high alcohol, soft tannin, generous. Full, warming, textured. McLaren Vale's secret.

Also Called: Garnacha (Spain), Grenache (France)
Typical Alcohol: 14–15%
Tannin: Low-Medium
Homes: McLaren Vale (Australia)
