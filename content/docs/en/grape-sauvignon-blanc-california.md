## Overview

White Grape / USA

Sauvignon Blanc (California)

California Sauvignon. Medium-high acidity, medium alcohol. Tropical, herb. Napa expression. The ripe California take.

Also Called: Sauvignon Blanc, Fumé Blanc
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Napa Valley (USA)
