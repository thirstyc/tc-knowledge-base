## Overview

Red Grape / Spain

Cencibel

Tempranillo with different character in La Mancha. Medium tannin, medium alcohol. Fresh, fruity. Mancha's workhorse. The everyday Spanish red.

Also Called: Tempranillo (La Mancha)
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: La Mancha (Spain)
