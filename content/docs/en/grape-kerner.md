## Overview

White Grape / Germany

Kerner

German hybrid. Medium acidity, low-medium alcohol. Citrus, floral. Fresh, aromatic. German innovation. The hybrid that tastes natural.

Also Called: Kerner
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Germany, Rheinhessen
