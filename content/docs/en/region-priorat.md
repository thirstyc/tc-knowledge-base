## Overview

Wine Region / Spain

Priorat

Dark, mineral, and powerful reds grown on steep terraced hillsides of black slate. Nearly abandoned by the 1970s, now one of only two Spanish regions with the top DOCa classification.

Key Grapes: Garnacha, Cariñena
Climate: Hot, dry Mediterranean
Known For: Llicorella slate soil, tiny yields, old vines
Homes: Catalonia, northeastern Spain
