## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Catarratto

Sicilian white. High acidity, medium alcohol. Fresh, mineral, citrus. Sicily's workhorse white. The everyday wine that tastes serious.

Also Called: Catarratto Bianco
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Sicily (Italy)
