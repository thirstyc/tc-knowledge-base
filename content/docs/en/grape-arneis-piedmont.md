## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Arneis

Piedmont white. Medium alcohol, medium acidity, almond notes. Hazelnut finish. Rare, nutty, elegant. The wine that tastes like nuts and flowers.

Also Called: Arneis Bianco
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Piedmont (Italy)
