## Overview

Red Grape / Chile

Cabernet Sauvignon (Chile)

Chilean Cab. Powerful, full-bodied, ripe fruit. Medium-high tannin, high alcohol. Food wine. Colchagua Valley signature. The New World powerhouse.

Also Called: Cabernet Sauvignon
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Colchagua Valley (Chile)
