## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Barolo (Nebbiolo)

Nebbiolo Barolo. Very high tannin, very high acidity. Powerful, structured, age-worthy. Piedmont royalty. The King of wines.

Also Called: Nebbiolo di Alba
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Barolo (Italy), Piedmont
