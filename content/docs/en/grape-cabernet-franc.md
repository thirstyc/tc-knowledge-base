## Overview

Red Grape / France, Canada, USA

Cabernet Franc

High acidity, low tannin, the food wine that doesn't announce itself. Bordeaux's supporting actor turned Loire Valley star. The grape that tastes like conversation over dinner.

Also Called: Bouchet, Breton (Loire)
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium-Low
Homes: Loire Valley (France), Bordeaux, Ontario (Canada), Napa Valley
