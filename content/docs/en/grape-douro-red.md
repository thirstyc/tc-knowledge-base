## Overview

Red Grape / Portugal

Douro Red

Douro blend. High tannin, high acidity. Structured, age-worthy. Touriga Franca, Touriga Nacional, Tinta Roriz. The Portuguese blend.

Also Called: Douro Red Blend
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Douro Valley (Portugal)
