## Overview

Red Grape / Germany

Spätburgunder

German Pinot Noir. High acidity, medium tannin, low alcohol. Elegant, not powerful. Cool-climate Pinot that shows restraint. The wine that taught Pinot drinkers about terroir.

Also Called: Pinot Noir (Germany)
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Mosel (Germany), Rheingau
