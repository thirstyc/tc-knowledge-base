## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Nero d'Avola

Sicilian red. High tannin, ripe fruit, minerality. Medium-high alcohol. Dark, structured, warming. Sicily's answer to serious reds. The island's terroir in a glass.

Also Called: Calabrese
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Sicily (Italy), Val di Noto
