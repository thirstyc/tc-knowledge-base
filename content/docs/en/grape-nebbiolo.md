## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Nebbiolo

Powerful, elegant, the king of Piedmont. High tannin, high acidity, needs age. This is wine that demands respect—from the maker and the drinker. Worth the wait.

Also Called: Nebbiolo d'Alba
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Piedmont (Italy), Barolo, Barbaresco
