## Overview

White Grape / Romania

Feteasca Alba

Romanian white. Medium acidity, medium alcohol. Floral, fresh. Dealul Mare. The Romanian white. Elegant, simple, good.

Also Called: Feteasca Alba
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Dealul Mare (Romania)
