## Overview

Red Grape / USA

Zinfandel (Old-Vine)

Old-vine California Zinfandel. High alcohol, high tannin, concentrated. Jammy, spicy, powerful. 80+ year vines. The heritage wine.

Also Called: Zinfandel Vecchio
Typical Alcohol: 15–16%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Paso Robles (USA), Dry Creek Valley
