## Overview

White Grape / Spain

Viura

Spanish white from Rioja. High acidity, medium alcohol. Fresh, citrus, mineral. Often blended with Malvasia. The Rioja white.

Also Called: Macabeo (Catalonia), Macabeu
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Rioja (Spain)
