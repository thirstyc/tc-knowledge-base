## Overview

White Grape / Spain

Verdejo

Castile white. High acidity, medium-high alcohol. Mineral, citrus, herbal. Age-worthy. Rueda's signature. The Spanish white that tastes like land.

Also Called: Verdejo Blanco
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Rueda (Spain)
