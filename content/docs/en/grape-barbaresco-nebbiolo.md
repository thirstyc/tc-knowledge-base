## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Barbaresco (Nebbiolo)

Nebbiolo Barbaresco. Very high tannin, very high acidity. Elegant, complex, age-worthy. Piedmont royalty. The Queen of wines.

Also Called: Nebbiolo di Alba
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Barbaresco (Italy), Piedmont
