## Overview

White Grape / Spain

Albarin

Galician white. Very high acidity, low alcohol. Mineral, saline. Rías Baixas sister to Albariño. Equally mineral, equally bracing.

Also Called: Albarín Blanco
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Galicia (Spain)
