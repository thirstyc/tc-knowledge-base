## Overview

Red Grape / Romania

Feteasca Neagra

Romanian red. High tannin, medium acidity. Dark, structured. Dealul Mare. The Romanian classic. Serious, food-worthy.

Also Called: Feteasca Neagra
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Dealul Mare (Romania)
