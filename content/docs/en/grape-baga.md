## Overview

Red Grape / Portugal

Baga

Portuguese powerhouse. Very high tannin, high acidity. Structured, age-worthy. Low alcohol (11–13%). Bairrada's signature. Needs time but worth the wait.

Also Called: Baga Tinta
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Bairrada (Portugal)
