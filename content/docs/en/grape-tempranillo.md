## Overview

Red Grape / Spain

Tempranillo

Rioja backbone. Age-worthy, oak-friendly, high tannin. The Spanish red workhorse. Modern vs traditional is the real story—fruit vs oak, now vs later.

Also Called: Tinto Fino, Tinta del País, Cencibel
Typical Alcohol: 14–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Rioja (Spain), Ribera del Duero (Spain), Priorat (Spain)
