## Overview

White Grape / New Zealand

Sauvignon Blanc (NZ)

New Zealand changed this grape. High acidity, tropical fruit, grapefruit, herbaceous. Low alcohol. Bright, loud, exciting. Marlborough's gift to wine world.

Also Called: Fumé Blanc
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Marlborough (New Zealand)
