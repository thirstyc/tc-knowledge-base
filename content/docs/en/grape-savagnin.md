## Overview

White Grape / France

Savagnin

Jura white. Medium acidity, medium-high alcohol. Nutty, complex, age-worthy. Under-the-veil oxidation. The puzzle wine.

Also Called: Traminer (different from Gewürz)
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Jura (France)
