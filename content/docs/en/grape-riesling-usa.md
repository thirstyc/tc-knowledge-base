## Overview

White Grape / USA

Riesling (USA)

American Riesling. Dry expression mostly. Medium acidity, medium alcohol. Finger Lakes and Washington State. Cool-climate finesse. Proof US Riesling deserves respect.

Also Called: Riesling
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Finger Lakes (USA), Washington State
