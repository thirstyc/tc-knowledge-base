## Overview

Sparkling / France

Champagne

Sparkling wine from Champagne. Traditional method. Chardonnay + Pinot Noir + Pinot Meunier blend. Bubbles, complexity, elegance. The celebration wine.

Also Called: Champagne
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Champagne (France)
