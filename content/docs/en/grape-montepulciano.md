## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Montepulciano

Central Italian red. High acidity, medium tannin, structured. Fruity, earthy, mineral. Age-worthy. Montepulciano d'Abruzzo's backbone. Serious wine at friendly price.

Also Called: Montepulciano d'Abruzzo
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Abruzzo (Italy), Montepulciano
