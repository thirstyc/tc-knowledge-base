## Overview

White Grape / Australia

Riesling (Australia)

Australian Riesling. High acidity, low-medium alcohol. Citrus, mineral. Clare Valley. Dry expression. Proof Australia can do mineral whites.

Also Called: Riesling
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Clare Valley (Australia)
