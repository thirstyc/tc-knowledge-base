## Overview

White Grape / France

Muscadet (Loire)

Loire Muscadet. Very high acidity, low alcohol. Crisp, mineral, oyster-friendly. Sur lie aging adds depth. The Loire legend.

Also Called: Melon de Bourgogne (Loire Valley)
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Muscadet Sèvre et Maine (Loire)
