## Overview

White Grape / Portugal

Vinho Verde

Sparkling white. Low alcohol (8–11%), high acidity, slightly sweet. Young, fresh, fun. The aperitif wine. Lightly carbonated. Portugal's gift to casual wine drinking.

Also Called: Vinho Verde (the wine name)
Typical Alcohol: 8–11%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Minho (Portugal)
