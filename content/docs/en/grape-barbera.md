## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Barbera

The people's wine. High acidity, medium tannin, drinks young, costs less. Piedmont's everyday red. Proof that seriousness and approachability aren't mutually exclusive.

Also Called: Barbera d'Alba, Barbera d'Asti
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Piedmont (Italy), California
