## Overview

Red Grape / France

Châteauneuf-du-Pape

13-grape blend (Grenache minimum 80%). Full-bodied, high tannin, high alcohol. The Rhône legend. Complex, age-worthy, expensive. The wine that built Southern Rhône's reputation.

Also Called: GSM (Grenache, Syrah, Mourvèdre)
Typical Alcohol: 14–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Southern Rhône (France)
