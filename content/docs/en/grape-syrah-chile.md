## Overview

Red Grape / Chile

Syrah (Chile)

Chilean Syrah. High tannin, high alcohol. Peppery, dark. Cool altitude ripening. The Chilean alternative to Cabernet.

Also Called: Syrah, Shiraz
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Central Chile, Colchagua
