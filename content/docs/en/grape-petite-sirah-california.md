## Overview

Red Grape / USA

Petite Sirah (California)

California Petite Sirah. Very high tannin, high alcohol. Dense, dark. Paso Robles specialty. The heavyweight US red.

Also Called: Petite Sirah, Durif
Typical Alcohol: 14–16%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Paso Robles (USA)
