## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Corvina

Northern Italian red. Medium tannin, high acidity. Fresh, cherry-forward. Valpolicella's backbone. Light enough to drink young, structured enough to age.

Also Called: Corvina Veronese
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Veneto (Italy), Valpolicella
