## Overview

White Grape / France

Sauternes

Sweet white wine grape. High sugar, noble rot. Medium acidity, concentrated, golden. The dessert wine that tastes like luxury. Semillon + Sauvignon Blanc blend.

Also Called: Semillon (primary grape)
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Bordeaux (France), Sauternes region
