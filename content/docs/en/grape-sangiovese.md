## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Sangiovese

High tannin, high acidity, earth-driven. The backbone of Italian wine. Tuscany didn't need anyone else to prove itself. Food wine disguised as a serious red.

Also Called: Brunello (Montalcino), Vino Nobile (Montepulciano)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Tuscany (Italy), Piedmont (Italy)
