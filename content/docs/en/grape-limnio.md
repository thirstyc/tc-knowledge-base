## Overview

Red Grape / Greece

Limnio

Greek red from Limnos. Medium tannin, medium acidity. Fresh, spicy. Ancient origin. Rare but age-worthy. The Greek mystery.

Also Called: Limnio
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Limnos (Greece)
