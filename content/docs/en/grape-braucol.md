## Overview

Red Grape / France

Braucol

Southwest France red. High tannin, high acidity. Structured, dark. Gaillac. The serious Southwest red. Proof that Southwest France is serious.

Also Called: Fer Servadou
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Southwest France, Gaillac
