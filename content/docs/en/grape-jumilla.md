## Overview

Red Grape / Spain

Jumilla

Spanish red from Murcia. High alcohol, medium tannin. Ripe fruit, warm. The Spanish everyday red. Affordable power.

Also Called: Jumilla Monastrell
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Jumilla (Spain)
