## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Garganega

Veneto white. High acidity, low alcohol. Citrus, almond. Light, fresh. Soave's base. The Italian Pinot Grigio alternative.

Also Called: Garganega di Gambellara
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Veneto (Italy), Soave
