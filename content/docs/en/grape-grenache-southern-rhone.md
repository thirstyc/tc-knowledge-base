## Overview

Red Grape / France

Grenache (Southern Rhône)

Southern Rhône Grenache. High alcohol, soft tannin, generous. Châteauneuf-du-Pape base. The Southern workhorse.

Also Called: Garnacha (Spain)
Typical Alcohol: 14–16%
Tannin: Low-Medium
Homes: Southern Rhône (France)
