## Overview

White Grape / Greece

Agiorgitiko (White)

Greek white. High acidity, medium alcohol. Fresh, mineral, citrus. The white sibling. Underrated, elegant, serious.

Also Called: Agiorgitiko Leuko
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Corinth (Greece)
