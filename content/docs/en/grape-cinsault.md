## Overview

Red Grape / France, Spain

Cinsault

Light, fruity red. Low tannin, high acidity. Often in blends. Warm-climate grape. Rosé specialist. Easy-drinking, refreshing, casual.

Also Called: Cinsaut, Cinsulo
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Low
Homes: Southern France, Provence
