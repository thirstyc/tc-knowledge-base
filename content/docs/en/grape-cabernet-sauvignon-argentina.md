## Overview

Red Grape / Argentina

Cabernet Sauvignon (Argentina)

Argentine Cabernet. High altitude ripens slowly. Medium-high tannin, structured, fruit-forward. Napa power meets Mendoza finesse. The alternative to California.

Also Called: Cabernet Sauvignon
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Mendoza (Argentina), Maipo Valley
