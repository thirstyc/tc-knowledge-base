## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Barbaresco

Nebbiolo from Piedmont. Very high tannin, very high acidity. Similar to Barolo but slightly more elegant. The Queen of Italian wines. Age-worthy, complex.

Also Called: Nebbiolo di Alba
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Piedmont (Italy), Barbaresco region
