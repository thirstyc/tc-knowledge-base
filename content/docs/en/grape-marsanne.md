## Overview

White Grape / France

Marsanne

Full-bodied Northern Rhône white. Medium acidity, waxy texture. Stone fruit, honey. Crozes-Hermitage. Drinks young but ages beautifully.

Also Called: Marsanne Blanche
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Northern Rhône (France)
