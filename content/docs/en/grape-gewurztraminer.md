## Overview

White Grape / France, Germany, Austria

Gewürztraminer

Aromatic, floral, often off-dry. Low acidity, medium alcohol. Lychee, roses, spice. The grape that smells like dessert but tastes like dinner. Polarizing—people love it or hate it.

Also Called: Traminer, Traminer Aromatico
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: Low-Medium
Homes: Alsace (France), Germany, Austria
