## Overview

Red Grape / France

Gamay (Fleurie)

Beaujolais cru Gamay. Low tannin, high acidity. Fresh, fruity, mineral. Proof that Gamay ages. The serious Beaujolais.

Also Called: Gamay Noir (Fleurie designation)
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Tannin: Low-Medium
Homes: Fleurie (France), Beaujolais Cru
