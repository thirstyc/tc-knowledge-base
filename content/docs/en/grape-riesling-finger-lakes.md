## Overview

White Grape / USA

Riesling (Finger Lakes)

Finger Lakes Riesling. Very high acidity, low alcohol. Dry or off-dry. Cool-climate finesse. The American Riesling masterclass.

Also Called: Riesling
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Finger Lakes (USA)
