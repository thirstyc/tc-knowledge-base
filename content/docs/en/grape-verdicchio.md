## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Verdicchio

Dry, mineral, age-worthy white. High acidity, stone fruit. Marche region's pride. Underrated because it doesn't shout, but the complexity is there if you listen.

Also Called: Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: High
Homes: Marche (Italy)
