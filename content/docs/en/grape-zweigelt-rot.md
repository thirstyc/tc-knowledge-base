## Overview

White Grape / Austria

Zweigelt (Rot)

Austrian red. Medium tannin, medium-high acidity. Spicy, structured. The Austrian red. Burgenland. Serious wine.

Also Called: Zweigelt Rot
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Burgenland (Austria)
