## Overview

Red Grape / USA, Argentina

Petite Sirah

California's powerhouse. High tannin, dense, full-bodied. High alcohol, dark fruit, peppery. Built for food and aging. The grape that makes you chew. Underrated outside California.

Also Called: Durif, Petit Sirah
Typical Alcohol: 14–16%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: California (USA), Paso Robles
