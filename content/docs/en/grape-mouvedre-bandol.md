## Overview

Red Grape / France

Mourvèdre (Bandol)

Bandol Mourvèdre. High tannin, high acidity, structured. Age-worthy. The Provence classic. Serious, mineral, powerful.

Also Called: Monastrell (Spain), Mataro (USA)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Bandol (France), Provence
