## Overview

White Grape / Portugal

Vinho Verde Green

Light, crisp, young. Low alcohol (8–11%), high acidity. Slightly sparkling. Youth is the point. Fresh, fun, affordable. The aperitif that changed Portuguese wine.

Also Called: Vinho Verde Branco
Typical Alcohol: 8–11%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Minho (Portugal), Douro Valley
