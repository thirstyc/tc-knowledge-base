## Overview

White Grape / Australia

Semillon

Australian Semillon. Medium alcohol, high acidity, waxy texture. Ages beautifully. Develops honey, toast over time. Hunter Valley icon. Seriously underrated.

Also Called: Semillon d'Australie
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: High
Homes: Hunter Valley (Australia)
