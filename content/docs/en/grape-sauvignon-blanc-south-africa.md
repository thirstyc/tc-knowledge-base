## Overview

White Grape / South Africa

Sauvignon Blanc (South Africa)

South African Sauvignon. High acidity, medium alcohol. Herbal, mineral. Elgin. The mineral alternative to New Zealand.

Also Called: Sauvignon Blanc
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Elgin (South Africa)
