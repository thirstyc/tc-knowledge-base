## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Aglianico

The southern Italian gem. High tannin, high acidity, powerful, age-worthy. Campania's best-kept secret. Dark, structured, earthy—the wine that tastes like its volcanic soil.

Also Called: Aglianico del Vulture
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Campania (Italy), Basilicata
