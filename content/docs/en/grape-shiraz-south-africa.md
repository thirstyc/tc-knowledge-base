## Overview

Red Grape / South Africa

Shiraz (South Africa)

South African Shiraz. Medium-high tannin, high alcohol. Peppery, structured. Swartland. The South African alternative to Australia.

Also Called: Shiraz, Syrah
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Swartland (South Africa)
