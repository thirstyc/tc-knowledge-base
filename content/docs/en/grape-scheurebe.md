## Overview

White Grape / Germany

Scheurebe

German white. Medium-high acidity, medium alcohol. Aromatic, citrus. Grapefruit. Fun, fresh, distinctive. The German surprise.

Also Called: Scheurebe
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Germany, Pfalz
