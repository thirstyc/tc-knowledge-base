## Overview

Red Grape / France, USA, Australia

Cabernet Sauvignon

The grape everyone knows. High tannin, high acidity, built for aging. Bordeaux's star, California's trophy, Australia's powerhouse. Structure over fruit. This is the wine that tastes like authority.

Also Called: Cab, Cab Sauv
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Bordeaux (France), Napa Valley (USA), Australia
