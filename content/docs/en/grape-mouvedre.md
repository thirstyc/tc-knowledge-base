## Overview

Red Grape / France

Mourvèdre

Warm-climate red. High tannin, high alcohol, structured. Earthy, peppery. Southern France and Bandol. Proof that heat doesn't mean jammy.

Also Called: Monastrell (Spain), Mataro (USA)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Provence (France), Bandol
