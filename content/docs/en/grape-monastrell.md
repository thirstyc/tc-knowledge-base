## Overview

Red Grape / Spain, Portugal

Monastrell

Full-bodied, structured red. High tannin, ripe fruit, earthy. Spain's Mourvèdre. Warm-climate grape that tastes like earth and sun. Underrated outside Spain and Southern France.

Also Called: Mourvèdre (France), Mataro (USA)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Murcia (Spain), Spain, France
