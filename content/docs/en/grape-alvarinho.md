## Overview

White Grape / Portugal

Alvarinho

Portuguese Albariño. Very high acidity, medium alcohol. Briny, citrus, mineral. Vinho Verde's fancy cousin. The serious Portuguese white.

Also Called: Albariño (Spain)
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Minho (Portugal)
