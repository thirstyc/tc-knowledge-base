## Overview

Red Grape / France

Gamay (Old-Vine)

Older Gamay vines add structure. Same grape as Beaujolais young, but age changes it. Medium tannin, complexity, depth. Proof that Gamay can age.

Also Called: Gamay Noir
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Beaujolais (France), Côte de Brouilly
