## Overview

White Grape / Argentina

Torrontés

Argentine white. High acidity, medium-high alcohol. Aromatic, floral. Salta high-altitude. The Argentine aromatic. Fresh, elegant, fun.

Also Called: Torrontés Riojano
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: High
Homes: Salta (Argentina)
