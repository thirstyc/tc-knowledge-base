## Overview

Red Grape / Chile

Carménère (Chile)

Chilean Carménère. Medium-high tannin, high alcohol. Ripe fruit, spice. Colchagua. Chile's flagship. The identity wine.

Also Called: Carménère
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Colchagua Valley (Chile)
