## Overview

Wine Region / Canada

Niagara Peninsula

Cool Lake Ontario climate sheltered by the escarpment, built for crisp Riesling and Cabernet Franc. An ice wine heritage now backing up a serious dry wine reputation.

Key Grapes: Riesling, Cabernet Franc, Vidal (ice wine)
Climate: Cool, lake-moderated continental
Known For: Ice wine, escarpment-sheltered vineyards
Homes: Ontario, Canada
