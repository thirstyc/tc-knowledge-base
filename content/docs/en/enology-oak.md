## Overview

How Wine Works

Oak

Barrels add vanilla, spice, toast, and texture to wine, and let in a tiny, controlled amount of oxygen that softens tannin over time. How much oak a wine sees — new versus old, French versus American — is one of the biggest style decisions a winemaker makes.

Adds: Vanilla, spice, toast, texture
Common Types: French oak, American oak
New vs Old: New oak adds more flavor, old oak adds less
Alternatives: Stainless steel, concrete, amphora
