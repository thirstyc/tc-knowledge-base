## Overview

White Grape / France

Chablis

Unoaked Chardonnay from Chablis. Mineral, lean, flinty. Very high acidity. Cool-climate expression of Chardonnay. The anti-California Chardonnay.

Also Called: Chardonnay (Chablis region)
Typical Alcohol: 11–12%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Chablis (France)
