## Overview

Red Grape / Greece

Agiorgitiko

Greek red from Corinth. High acidity, medium-high tannin. Structured, age-worthy. Dark fruit, spice. The Pinot Noir of Greece.

Also Called: St. George (English), Agiorgitiko
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Corinth (Greece)
