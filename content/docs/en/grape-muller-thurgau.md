## Overview

White Grape / Germany

Müller-Thurgau

Light, fruity, aromatic. Low alcohol, medium acidity. Easy-drinking. Alsace's secret weapon. Proof that simplicity is underrated. Drink young, chill down.

Also Called: Rivaner (Austria)
Typical Alcohol: 11–12%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Germany, Alsace (France)
