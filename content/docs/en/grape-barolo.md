## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Barolo

Nebbiolo from Piedmont. Very high tannin, very high acidity. Medium-high alcohol. Powerful, elegant, age-worthy. The King of Italian wines. Demands respect and time.

Also Called: Nebbiolo d'Alba (different region)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Piedmont (Italy), Barolo region
