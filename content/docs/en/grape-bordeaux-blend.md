## Overview

Red Grape / France

Bordeaux Blend

Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc, Petit Verdot, Malbec. The classic blend. Medium-high tannin, high acidity. The wine that built Bordeaux's reputation. The template for New World reds.

Also Called: Claret (UK), Meritage (USA)
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Bordeaux (France)
