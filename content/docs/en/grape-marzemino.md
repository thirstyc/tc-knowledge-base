## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Marzemino

Northern Italian red. Medium tannin, medium acidity. Fresh, cherry. Trentino. Simple, approachable, delicious. The Italian weeknight red.

Also Called: Marzemino Veronese
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Trentino (Italy)
