## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Chianti

Tuscany's backbone wine. Sangiovese-based. Medium tannin, medium acidity. Food wine, everyday wine, serious wine. The pizza-and-pasta wine that tastes like Tuscany.

Also Called: Chianti Classico (DOCG designation)
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Tuscany (Italy), Chianti region
