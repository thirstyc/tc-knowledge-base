## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Refosco (Italy)

Friuli red. High acidity, medium tannin. Fresh, peppery. Alpine character. The Friuli red. Serious wine.

Also Called: Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Friuli (Italy)
