## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Fiano

Full-bodied Campanian white. Medium alcohol, medium acidity. Nutty, mineral, textured. The northern Italian white that drinks like a serious wine. Age-worthy if kept cool.

Also Called: Fiano di Avellino, Greco Fiano
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Campania (Italy), Avellino
