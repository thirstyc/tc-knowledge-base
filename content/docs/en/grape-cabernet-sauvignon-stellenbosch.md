## Overview

Red Grape / South Africa

Cabernet Sauvignon (Stellenbosch)

Stellenbosch Cabernet. High tannin, medium-high alcohol. Ripe fruit, power. The South African classic.

Also Called: Cabernet Sauvignon
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Stellenbosch (South Africa)
