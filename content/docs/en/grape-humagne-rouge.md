## Overview

Red Grape / Switzerland

Humagne Rouge

Swiss red. High acidity, medium tannin. Fresh, structured. Alpine red. The mountain wine. Rare, interesting, elegant.

Also Called: Humagne Rouge
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Switzerland, Valais
