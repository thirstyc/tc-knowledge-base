## Overview

White Grape / Slovenia

Rebula

Slovenian white. High acidity, medium alcohol. Mineral, citrus. Vipava Valley. The Slovenian mineral white.

Also Called: Ribolla Gialla (Italy)
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Vipava Valley (Slovenia)
