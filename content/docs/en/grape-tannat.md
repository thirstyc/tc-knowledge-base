## Overview

Red Grape / Uruguay, Argentina, Spain

Tannat

High tannin, high structure, the heavyweight. Needs age or food. Uruguay's pride. Pounded your gums with tannin when young—that's the point. Give it time.

Also Called: Harriague (Uruguay)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Uruguay, Argentina, Southwest France
