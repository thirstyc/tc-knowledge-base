## Overview

Red Grape / Argentina

Malbec (Salta)

Salta Malbec. High altitude = cool ripening. Dark, structured. The high-altitude expression. Pure, elegant, powerful.

Also Called: Malbec
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: High
Homes: Salta (Argentina)
