## Overview

White Grape / Hungary

Furmint

Hungarian white. Very high acidity, medium alcohol. Mineral, citrus, age-worthy. Tokaji base. Dry expression is elegant.

Also Called: Hárslevelű (blend partner)
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Tokaji (Hungary)
