## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Friulano

Friuli white. High acidity, medium alcohol. Fresh, mineral. Crisp, herbal. Tocai Friulano. The Friuli workhorse.

Also Called: Tocai Friulano
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Friuli (Italy)
