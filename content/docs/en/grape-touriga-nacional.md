## Overview

Red Grape / Spain, Portugal

Touriga Nacional

Portuguese red. High tannin, structured, age-worthy. Dark fruit, floral notes. High alcohol. Port's king grape, but excellent dry wine too. Portugal's flagship red.

Also Called: Touriga
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Douro Valley (Portugal)
