## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Insolia

Sicilian white. Medium acidity, medium alcohol, mineral. Dry, textured. Often blended but excellent alone. The wine that tastes like Sicily.

Also Called: Ansonica
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Sicily (Italy)
