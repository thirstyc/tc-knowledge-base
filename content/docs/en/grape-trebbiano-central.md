## Overview

White Grape / Italy

Trebbiano

Central Italian white. High acidity, low alcohol, clean. Neutral, crisp. Brandy base. The working white. Proof that simple works.

Also Called: Trebbiano d'Italia
Typical Alcohol: 10–12%
Acidity: High
Homes: Central Italy (Tuscany, Umbria)
