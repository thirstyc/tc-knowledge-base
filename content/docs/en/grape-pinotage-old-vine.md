## Overview

Red Grape / South Africa

Pinotage (Old-Vine)

Old-vine Pinotage adds complexity. Medium tannin, structured, age-worthy. Same unique character but with depth. 20+ years shows the wine's true power.

Also Called: Pinotage
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Stellenbosch (South Africa)
