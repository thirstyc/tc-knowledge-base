## Overview

White Grape / Lebanon

Cinsault (Lebanon)

Lebanese white. Medium acidity, medium alcohol. Fresh, mineral. Bekaa Valley. The Middle Eastern expression. Surprising elegance.

Also Called: Cinsault Blanc
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Bekaa Valley (Lebanon)
