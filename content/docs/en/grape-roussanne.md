## Overview

White Grape / France

Roussanne

Silky Rhône white. Medium alcohol, high acidity, floral. Elegant, mineral. Hermitage. The white that tastes like flowers and stone.

Also Called: Roussanne
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: High
Homes: Northern Rhône (France)
