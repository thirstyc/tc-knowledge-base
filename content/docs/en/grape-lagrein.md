## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Lagrein

Alto Adige's full-bodied red. Medium-high tannin, dark fruit, spice. High-altitude mountain wine that tastes like where it's from. Underrated, distinctive, worth seeking out.

Also Called: Lagrein Dunkel
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Alto Adige (Italy)
