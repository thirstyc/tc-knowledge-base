## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Tocai (Italy)

Italian Friuli red. Medium acidity, medium-high alcohol. Fresh, mineral. Often called Sauvignon in other places. The Friuli enigma.

Also Called: Tocai Friulano
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Friuli (Italy)
