## Overview

White Grape / France

Gros Manseng

Southwest France white. High acidity, low-medium alcohol. Dry, mineral. Madiran region. The elegant Southwest white.

Also Called: Gros Manseng
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Southwest France, Béarn
