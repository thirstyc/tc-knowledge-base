## Overview

Red Grape / Greece

Xinomavro

Greek red. Very high acidity, very high tannin. Dark, structured, age-worthy. The wine that tastes like ancient Greece. Needs food. Built to last.

Also Called: Xinomagro
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Naoussa (Greece)
