Red Grape / Spain

Tinta de Toro

Spanish Tempranillo. High tannin, medium-high alcohol. Powerful, structured. Toro region. The Spanish powerhouse.

Also Called: Tinta de Toro
Typical Alcohol: 14–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Toro (Spain)
