## Overview

Red Grape / France

Syrah (Northern Rhône)

Northern Rhône Syrah. High acidity, medium-high tannin. Elegant, mineral, peppery. Côte-Rôtie. The Northern showcase.

Also Called: Syrah
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Côte-Rôtie (France), Northern Rhône
