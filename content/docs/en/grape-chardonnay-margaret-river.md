## Overview

White Grape / Australia

Chardonnay (Margaret River)

Margaret River Chardonnay. Medium-high alcohol, medium acidity. Stone fruit, oak-influenced. The oaked Australian expression.

Also Called: Chardonnay
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Margaret River (Australia)
