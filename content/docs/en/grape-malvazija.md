## Overview

White Grape / Croatia

Malvazija

Istrian white. Medium acidity, medium-high alcohol. Mineral, floral. Fresh. Croatian coastal white. Proof that Adriatic tastes different.

Also Called: Malvasia
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Istria (Croatia)
