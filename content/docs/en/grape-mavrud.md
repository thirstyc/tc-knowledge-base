## Overview

Red Grape / Bulgaria

Mavrud

Bulgarian red. High tannin, high acidity. Structured, spicy. Ancient Thracian grape. Age-worthy, complex. The Bulgarian treasure.

Also Called: Mavrud
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Thrace (Bulgaria)
