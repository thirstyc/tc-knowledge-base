## Overview

How Wine Works

Wine Faults

The difference between a flawed bottle and a stylistic choice is one of the most useful things to learn as a drinker. Cork taint never clears with air; reduction often does.

Most Common: Cork taint (TCA), oxidation, volatile acidity
Smells Like: Wet cardboard, vinegar, rotten egg
Sometimes Fixable: Reduction can blow off with a decant
Never Fixable: Cork taint (TCA)
