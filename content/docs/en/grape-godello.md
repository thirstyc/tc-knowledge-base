## Overview

White Grape / Spain

Godello

Mineral, crisp, high-altitude. Galicia's answer to Albariño but with more structure. Medium alcohol, high acidity. The thinking person's white. Needs no oak, no fuss, just terroir.

Also Called: Godello Blanco
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Galicia (Spain), Valdeorras
