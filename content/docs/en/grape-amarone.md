## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Amarone

Dried-grape Valpolicella wine. High alcohol (15–17%), concentrated, complex. Raisiny, rich, textured. The Italian dessert wine that tastes like dinner. Expensive, age-worthy.

Also Called: Recioto della Valpolicella Amarone
Typical Alcohol: 15–17%
Tannin: High
Homes: Veneto (Italy), Valpolicella
