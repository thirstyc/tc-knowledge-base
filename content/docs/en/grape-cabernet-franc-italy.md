## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Cabernet Franc (Italy)

Italian Cabernet Franc. Medium tannin, medium acidity. Fresh, herbaceous. Friuli and Northeast. The Italian expression.

Also Called: Cabernet Franc
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Friuli, Veneto (Italy)
