## Overview

Red Grape / Croatia

Plavac Mali

Croatian red. High tannin, high acidity. Spicy, structured. Dalmatian coast. The red that tastes like sun and stone.

Also Called: Plavac Mali
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Dalmatia (Croatia)
