## Overview

White Grape / USA

Chardonnay (Sonoma)

Sonoma Chardonnay. Medium alcohol, medium-high acidity. Stone fruit, mineral. Cooler expression. The elegant California Chardonnay.

Also Called: Chardonnay
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Medium-High
Homes: Sonoma Coast (USA)
