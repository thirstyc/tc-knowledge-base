## Overview

White Grape / USA

Pinot Grigio (Oregon)

Oregon Pinot Grigio. Medium acidity, medium alcohol. Fuller than Italian. Stone fruit, texture. The rich American version.

Also Called: Pinot Gris (Oregon designation)
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Willamette Valley (USA)
