## Overview

Red Grape / Portugal

Tinta Roriz

Portuguese red. High tannin, high acidity. Structured, dark fruit. Douro blend staple. Serious, age-worthy. The Portuguese classic.

Also Called: Tempranillo (Spain)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Douro Valley (Portugal)
