## Overview

Red Grape / USA

Pinot Noir (California)

California Pinot. Medium tannin, high acidity. Ripe cherry, structured. Cooler California sites. The bridge between Burgundy and California.

Also Called: Pinot Noir
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Sonoma Coast (USA)
