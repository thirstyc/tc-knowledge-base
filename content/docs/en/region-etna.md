## Overview

Wine Region / Italy

Etna

Vines planted in black volcanic ash on the slopes of an active volcano. Nerello Mascalese reds land somewhere between Pinot Noir and Nebbiolo — Sicily's most exciting wine story of the last two decades.

Key Grapes: Nerello Mascalese, Carricante
Climate: Volcanic, high-altitude Mediterranean
Known For: Volcanic soil, rapidly rising prices
Homes: Sicily, Italy
