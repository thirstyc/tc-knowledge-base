## Overview

White Grape / USA

Gewürztraminer (Washington)

Washington Gewürz. Medium-high alcohol, low acidity. Floral, spicy. Riper than Europe. The New World aromatic.

Also Called: Gewürztraminer
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Acidity: Low
Homes: Washington State (USA)
