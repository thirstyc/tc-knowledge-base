## Overview

Red Grape / France

Gamay

Low tannin, high acidity, the Beaujolais specialist. Red wine that feels like breakfast. Fermentation is the wine, not the grape. Terroir tastes like place, not fruit.

Also Called: Gamay Noir, Gamay de Chaudenay
Typical Alcohol: 11–13%
Tannin: Low
Homes: Beaujolais (France)
