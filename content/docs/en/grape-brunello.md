## Overview

Red Grape / Italy

Brunello

Sangiovese di Montalcino. High tannin, high acidity, age-worthy. 5-year minimum aging required. Structured, powerful, elegant. Tuscan royalty.

Also Called: Sangiovese Grosso
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Tuscany (Italy), Montalcino
