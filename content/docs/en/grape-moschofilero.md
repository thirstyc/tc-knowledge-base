## Overview

White Grape / Greece

Moschofilero

Greek white. High acidity, medium alcohol, aromatic. Floral, citrus notes. Cool-climate white from high altitude. Proof that Greece makes serious whites.

Also Called: Moschofilero
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Mantinia (Greece)
