## Overview

Red Grape / Spain

Prieto Picudo

Castile-León red. High tannin, high acidity. Structured, age-worthy, dark. Ribera del Duero's less famous sibling. Serious wine.

Also Called: Prieto Picudo
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Castile-León (Spain)
