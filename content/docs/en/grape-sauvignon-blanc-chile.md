## Overview

White Grape / Chile

Sauvignon Blanc (Chile)

Chilean Sauvignon. High acidity, medium alcohol. Tropical, grassy. Casablanca. The ripe Sauvignon expression.

Also Called: Sauvignon Blanc
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Acidity: High
Homes: Casablanca Valley (Chile)
