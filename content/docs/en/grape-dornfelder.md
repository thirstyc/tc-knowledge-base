## Overview

Red Grape / France

Dornfelder

German-origin red, grown in France. Medium-high tannin, medium acidity. Dark fruit, juicy. Warm-climate grape. The German innovation that travels.

Also Called: Dornfelder
Typical Alcohol: 13–14%
Tannin: Medium-High
Homes: Southern France, Germany
