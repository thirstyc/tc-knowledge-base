## Catalunya — overview {#catalunya-overview}

Catalunya (Catalonia), in Spain's northeast around Barcelona, is a diverse wine region spanning cool coastal and high inland zones. It's home to Spain's sparkling-wine industry (Cava), the powerful reds of Priorat, and a patchwork of DOs (Penedès, Montsant, Empordà, Costers del Segre, Terra Alta). Catalan wine culture is independent-minded and quality-focused.
Recommend when: the user wants Spanish sparkling, powerful old-vine reds, or a diverse region near Barcelona.

## Priorat {#priorat}

Priorat, a small, dramatic, mountainous DOCa inland from Tarragona, is one of Spain's two top-tier regions (with Rioja). Its signature is llicorella — a slate-and-quartz soil that forces old-vine Garnacha (Garnatxa) and Cariñena (Carinyena/Samsó) roots deep, yielding tiny crops of intensely concentrated, powerful, mineral reds. Often blended with Syrah or Cabernet in modern examples. Dark, dense, structured, and age-worthy — Spain's cult red region since the 1990s revival.
Serve at: 16–18°C.
Recommend when: the user wants a powerful, mineral, concentrated red; a special-occasion Spanish red; or old-vine Garnacha/Cariñena at its most intense.

## Montsant {#montsant}

Montsant wraps around Priorat and offers a similar profile — old-vine Garnacha and Cariñena reds, warm, concentrated, mineral — at friendlier prices. The go-to value alternative to Priorat.
Serve at: 16–18°C.
Recommend when: the user wants Priorat character without the price.

## Cava {#cava}

Cava is Spain's traditional-method sparkling wine — made the same way as Champagne (second fermentation in bottle, lees ageing) — centered in Catalunya's Penedès but a denomination rather than a single place. The traditional grapes are Macabeo (Viura), Xarel·lo, and Parellada, with Chardonnay and Pinot Noir also permitted (and Garnacha/Trepat for rosé). Ranges from simple, affordable fizz to serious, long-aged bottlings (Reserva, Gran Reserva, and the premium Cava de Paraje Calificado). A quality-focused breakaway group makes wine under the Corpinnat name.
Serve at: 8°C.
Recommend when: the user wants affordable traditional-method sparkling; a Champagne alternative; an apéritif or celebration wine on a budget.

## Penedès (still wines) {#pened-s-still-wines}

Beyond Cava, Penedès makes still whites and reds from both local and international grapes, across a range of elevations. Historically a modernizing region (early to plant Cabernet, Chardonnay, etc.), now also revaluing indigenous Xarel·lo for characterful whites.
Recommend when: the user wants a Catalan still white or an international-grape blend near Barcelona.

## Other Catalan DOs {#other-catalan-dos}

- Empordà — near the French border; Garnacha/Cariñena reds and rosés, some influenced by the Tramuntana wind.
- Costers del Segre — inland, varied; international and local grapes.
- Terra Alta — a stronghold of white Garnacha Blanca and old-vine reds; value and character.

Recommend when: the user wants a Catalan white Garnacha (Terra Alta) or a lesser-known regional red.

## Central Spain — La Mancha & the meseta {#central-spain-la-mancha-the-meseta}

La Mancha, the vast high plateau (meseta) south of Madrid, is the largest single wine zone in the world by area — hot, dry, and continental. Historically a source of bulk wine from the pale Airén grape (much distilled for brandy), it now makes increasingly fruity, stylish reds from Tempranillo (Cencibel), plus international grapes. Also home to several Vino de Pago single estates. Best known for value.
Recommend when: the user wants inexpensive, fruity, everyday Spanish reds or whites, or is curious about Spain's bulk-wine heartland going upmarket.

## Central Spain — Gredos Garnacha {#central-spain-gredos-garnacha}

The high Sierra de Gredos, west of Madrid, has become a cult source of elegant, fresh, perfumed Garnacha from old bushvines on granite at altitude — a lighter, more mineral, Burgundian style that helped rehabilitate Garnacha's fine-wine reputation. (Spans the Madrid/Castilla-La Mancha/Castilla y León borders.)
Serve at: 15–16°C.
Recommend when: the user wants an elegant, high-altitude, fresh style of Garnacha — a contrast to warm, jammy versions.

## Eastern Spain — Monastrell country {#eastern-spain-monastrell-country}

Inland from the central Mediterranean coast, hot regions once known for bulk wine now make serious, affordable reds — above all from Monastrell (Mourvèdre):

- Jumilla — the standard-bearer; ripe, powerful, dark Monastrell, often great value, increasingly polished.
- Yecla, Alicante, Bullas — similar warm-climate Monastrell reds; Alicante also has the historic sweet Fondillón (an oxidative, oloroso-like Monastrell).
- Utiel-Requena and Manchuela — high plateaus where the beefy Bobal grape dominates; increasingly characterful.
- Valencia — reds, whites, and sweet Moscatel.

Serve at: 16–18°C.
Recommend when: the user wants a powerful, warm, affordable red (Jumilla Monastrell), or a value alternative to Châteauneuf-style GSM.

## The Canary Islands {#the-canary-islands}

The volcanic Canary Islands, off northwest Africa, are a unique viticultural world: ungrafted vines (phylloxera never arrived) in black volcanic ash, often in dramatic pits or low trained against the wind (as in Lanzarote's La Geria). They grow a trove of rare indigenous grapes — Listán Negro (reds), Listán Blanco, Malvasía — making fresh, distinctive, mineral, sometimes smoky wines that have become sommelier favorites. Tenerife (with El Teide volcano) leads the revival.
Serve at: 13–16°C reds; 9–11°C whites.
Recommend when: the user wants something unique, volcanic, and mineral; a rare-grape adventure; or a distinctive natural-leaning wine.

## Quick regional summary spain-central-east {#quick-regional-summary-spain-central-east}

- Catalunya: diverse northeast; Priorat (powerful llicorella-slate Garnacha/Cariñena, DOCa) and value Montsant; Cava sparkling (Macabeo/Xarel·lo/Parellada); Penedès still wines; Terra Alta white Garnacha.
- Central (meseta): La Mancha — vast, hot, value reds/whites (Airén, Cencibel); Gredos — cult high-altitude elegant Garnacha.
- East: Monastrell power in Jumilla/Yecla/Alicante/Bullas; Bobal in Utiel-Requena/Manchuela; sweet Fondillón and Moscatel.
- Canary Islands: volcanic, ungrafted, rare indigenous grapes (Listán); fresh, mineral, unique.
