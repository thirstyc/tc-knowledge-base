## Overview

Red Grape / Bordeaux Region

Petit Verdot

The blend component that adds power. High tannin, high acidity, usually 5–10% of Bordeaux blends. The iron fist in a silk glove. More is not better; balance is everything.

Also Called: Petit Verdot
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Bordeaux (France), USA, Australia
