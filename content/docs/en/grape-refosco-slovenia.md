## Overview

Red Grape / Slovenia

Refosco

Slovenian red. High acidity, medium tannin. Fresh, peppery. Vipava Valley. The alpine red. Proof that cool mountains make serious wine.

Also Called: Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Slovenia, Vipava Valley
