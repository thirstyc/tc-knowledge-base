## Overview

Red Grape / Canada

Cabernet Franc (Ontario)

Ontario Cabernet Franc. High acidity, medium tannin. Fresh, herbaceous. Cool-climate expression. The Canadian cool.

Also Called: Cabernet Franc
Typical Alcohol: 12–13%
Tannin: Medium
Homes: Niagara Peninsula (Canada)
