## Overview

Red Grape / Hungary

Kadarka

Hungarian red. Very high tannin, high acidity. Spicy, mineral, age-worthy. Szekszárd. The Hungarian wine that tastes like history.

Also Called: Kadarka
Typical Alcohol: 12–14%
Tannin: Very High
Homes: Szekszárd (Hungary)
