## Overview

White Grape / France

Picpoul Blanc

Crisp, salty coastal white. High acidity, low alcohol. Languedoc's hidden gem. Seafood wine. Mineral, briny, fresh. Seriously underrated.

Also Called: Piquepoul Blanc, Picpoul
Typical Alcohol: 11–12%
Acidity: Very High
Homes: Languedoc (France)
