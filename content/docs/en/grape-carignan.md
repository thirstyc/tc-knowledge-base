## Overview

Red Grape / France

Carignan

Rustic, high-tannin red. Medium-high alcohol, earthy, spicy. Old-vine expressions show complexity. Languedoc and Southern France. The workhouse grape with soul.

Also Called: Cariñena (Spain), Carignane (USA)
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Tannin: High
Homes: Southern France, Languedoc, Spain
