## Overview

White Grape / Spain

Pajarete

Spanish white. Medium-high alcohol, medium acidity. Sweet or dry. Jerez region. The Spanish mystery white. Rare, traditional.

Also Called: Pajarete
Typical Alcohol: 13–15%
Acidity: Medium
Homes: Jerez (Spain)
