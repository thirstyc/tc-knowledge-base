What pairs with Paarl Chenin Blanc?

Short Answer

Paarl Chenin Blanc has enough weight and acid to actually stand up to a meal, not just precede one. The grape's natural acidity cuts through richness while the fruit and texture, often from old bush vines and sometimes a touch of barrel aging, give it body to match heartier dishes. Roast chicken works because the wine's acid balances the fat and its stone-fruit and honeyed notes echo the caramelized skin. Grilled prawns benefit from the same cut of acidity against char and butter. Creamy pasta, especially anything with cream, cheese, or a beurre blanc, needs a wine that won't get flattened, and Chenin's structure holds its own without turning sharp or thin. Serve it around 10-12°C, slightly warmer than a lean Sauvignon Blanc, so the texture and fruit actually show. This isn't a wine for a quick aperitif glass; it's built for the table, which is exactly why Paarl growers have leaned into it for generations.
