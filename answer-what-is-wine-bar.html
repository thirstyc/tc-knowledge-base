What is a wine bar?

Topic

Natural Wine

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

A wine bar is a bar focused on wine, usually with a large selection by the glass, knowledgeable staff and small plates to share. It's one of the best places to taste widely without buying whole bottles.

What sets a good wine bar apart:

  • A long by-the-glass list, often with tasting pours or flights of several small glasses.
  • Staff who can describe wines and recommend something you'll like.
  • Wine-friendly food: cheese, charcuterie, bread, olives and small dishes.
  • A selection that changes often, so there's always something new.

Wine bars come in many styles, from classic French and Italian enotecas to modern natural wine bars focused on small producers and skin-contact wines.

Many use preservation systems that let them pour expensive bottles by the glass, a chance to try wines you'd rarely open at home.

How to make the most of a visit:

  • Order a flight to compare grapes or regions side by side.
  • Tell the staff what you usually enjoy and let them surprise you.
  • Take a photo of labels you like.
  • Ask what just opened.

Some wine bars also sell bottles to take home, and many charge a reasonable price to drink a bottle on site.
