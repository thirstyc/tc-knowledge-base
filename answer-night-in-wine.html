Wine for a casual night in?

Short Answer

Whatever you'd genuinely enjoy with what you're eating. A casual night in is the best time to open an easy, reliable bottle you like, or to try something new without pressure.

Great everyday choices:

  • Red: Côtes du Rhône, Beaujolais, Montepulciano d'Abruzzo, a Chilean Carmenère or a Portuguese red, all soft, fruity and affordable.
  • White: Sauvignon Blanc, Picpoul de Pinet, Pinot Grigio or an unoaked Chardonnay.
  • Rosé: a dry rosé for warmer nights.
  • Sparkling: a Crémant or Cava, because Tuesday deserves bubbles too.

Match it loosely to dinner:

  • Pizza or pasta: Barbera, Chianti or Montepulciano d'Abruzzo.
  • Takeout curry or Thai: off-dry Riesling.
  • Burgers: Zinfandel or Malbec.
  • Sushi: Sauvignon Blanc or sparkling wine.
  • Just snacks and a film: something light and fruity.

A few habits that make weeknight wine better:

  • Keep a couple of everyday bottles on hand.
  • Re-cork and refrigerate what's left; most wine keeps three to five days.
  • Pour a measured glass rather than a big one.

It's also the perfect time to open a half bottle, or a style you've been curious about.
