What's the Difference Between Chianti, Brunello, and Vino Nobile?

Topic

Answers

Difficulty

Intermediate

Short Answer

Same grape (Sangiovese). Different regions = different aging requirements = different prices. Chianti is entry-level (3-4 year aging required, $10–20). Vino Nobile is mid-tier (2-year aging, $20–40). Brunello is premium (5-year aging, $30–100+). All serious. Brunello is just more expensive. Try Chianti first.
