What pairs with Touriga Nacional?

Short Answer

Touriga Nacional is built out of small, thick-skinned berries that pack in tannin and dark colour even before you get to the alcohol, which in warm Douro sites regularly climbs past 13.5%. That combination needs food with real fat and protein to soften it: think grilled lamb chops, a slow-cooked beef stew, or duck with something sweet like fig or prune to echo the grape's dark-fruit side. Salt-cured meats and hard cheeses also work because salt tames tannin's grip on your palate. Avoid delicate fish or anything creamy without acidity, the tannin will flatten it and taste metallic. Serve at 16-18°C, closer to the cooler end if the wine is young and still a bit aggressive. If you have a bottle from an off-vintage or younger producer, decant it thirty minutes ahead; the tannins soften noticeably with a little air, and the fruit opens up instead of hiding behind structure.
