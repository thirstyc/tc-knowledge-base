What is the best Chianti?

Topic

Buying Wine

Difficulty

Beginner

Short Answer

For most people, the best Chianti is a Chianti Classico from a good producer. It comes from the historic heart of the region and is a step up in depth and structure from basic Chianti.

The hierarchy to know:

  • Chianti: the broad appellation, fresh and fruity, best young and inexpensive.
  • Chianti sub-zones, like Chianti Rufina and Colli Senesi: often more character, with Rufina known for elegant, age-worthy wines.
  • Chianti Classico: the original zone between Florence and Siena, marked by the black rooster symbol.
  • Chianti Classico Riserva: aged longer, with more depth.
  • Chianti Classico Gran Selezione: the top tier, from a single estate's best grapes.

Gran Selezione labels can now also name one of eleven village areas, called UGAs, such as Radda, Gaiole, Castellina and Panzano, reflecting a growing focus on place.

Styles vary. Traditional producers make paler, more savoury wine with sour cherry and dried herbs; modern ones use more oak and riper fruit.

Price tip: Chianti Classico from a good year is one of the best-value serious reds in Italy.

Serve it with pasta in tomato sauce, pizza, grilled steak and aged Pecorino.
