Why does Lebanese Cabernet taste so structured?

Short Answer

The Bekaa Valley sits at high altitude between two mountain ranges, giving hot days and cool nights. Bordeaux grapes planted generations ago now taste distinctly their own. Old-world structure from one of the oldest wine regions on Earth.
